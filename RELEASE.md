The Math 2.0 Handbook, edition 0.1.1: the first two areas of [math2.tamnd.com](https://math2.tamnd.com) as books,
one volume per area, in PDF (7 × 10 in, with bookmarks and links), EPUB 3 and LaTeX source. Built from
[`tamnd/math2`](https://github.com/tamnd/math2) release v0.1.1 (`841862ade91c9704a84d0fcfe8e52f29d46d484e`); the
edition number follows the release of the site.

- **Volume 1, Reasoning and proof**, all fourteen chapters written: proof, theorems and mathematical method; the
  language of mathematical statements; definitions; notation, conventions and jargon; basic proof methods
  (direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction, cases, existence and uniqueness); induction and well-ordering;
  counting, invariant and extremal arguments; proof patterns for common goals; writing and reading proofs;
  fallacies and errors in proofs; heuristics of discovery; plausible reasoning and experimental mathematics; rigour
  and the acceptance of proofs; and the teaching of reasoning and proof.
- **Volume 2, Mathematical logic**, chapters 2.1 to 2.11 written: logic, formal systems and foundations;
  propositions, connectives and quantifiers; the propositional calculus; first-order languages and syntax;
  structures, satisfaction and truth; proof systems for first-order logic; completeness, compactness and
  interpolation; second-order logic and extensions of first-order logic; decidability of logics and theories;
  formal arithmetic and its fragments; Gödel's incompleteness theorems and related limits. Chapters 2.12 to 2.28
  (proof theory, provability logic, reverse mathematics, bounded arithmetic, proof complexity, modal,
  intuitionistic, many-valued, substructural and other non-classical logics, algebraic logic, truth and the
  paradoxes) have an opening each, saying what the planned pages will cover and what to read meanwhile.
- **Entries** are the pages of each sub-area's reading order on the site, numbered (2.7.3), each with its summary,
  a box of facts (other names, the pages it uses, MSC), motivation, notation, remarks, and three explanations at
  rising levels: *in plain words*, *in detail* and *for the specialist*. The worked examples and counterexamples
  of an entry follow it in boxes, and each entry ends with its sources.
- **Learner pages** around the entries: a volume introduction with a first-course path, and for each chapter an
  opening, groups with short introductions, a summary with tables, 20 to 28 exercises in three bands (◦ check,
  • practice, ★ challenge) with hints, and notes on history and further reading.
- **Apparatus**: a bibliography of the books, articles and preprints the entries cite, with the entries that cite
  each; and an index of entries, other names, and the pages of other areas that the entries use, with links to the
  site.

Assets: per volume, the PDF, the EPUB and the LaTeX source (`-tex.tar.gz`: `main.tex`, `body.tex`,
`handbook.sty`, built with tectonic); all PDFs and all EPUBs as two zips; and `SHA256SUMS`.

The learner pages were drafted by AI agents from a written brief and checked; see
