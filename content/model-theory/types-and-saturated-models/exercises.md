1. ◦ In $(\Z, <)$ let $A = \set{0, 3}$, and write $\delta_n(u, v)$ for the formula "at least $n$ elements lie strictly between $u$ and $v$". For each set of $L_A$-formulas, decide whether it is a [type](/9V71Q3SD1G/) over $A$ and, if it is, whether $(\Z, <)$ realizes it. (a) $\set{0 < x, x < 3}$. (b) $\set{3 < x} \cup \set{\delta_n(3, x) : n \in \N}$. (c) $\set{0 < x, x < 3, \delta_1(0, x), \delta_1(x, 3)}$. (d) $\set{x < 0} \cup \set{\delta_n(x, 0) : n \in \N}$. How many complete types over $A$ contain the set in (a)?

2. ◦ In $(\Q, <)$ let $A = \set{0, 1, 3}$. List the complete $1$-types over $A$, and say which of them are realized in $\Q$ and which are isolated points of $S_1(A)$. How many complete $1$-types are there over a set of $k$ rationals?

3. ◦ Let $T$ be the theory of an infinite set in the language with equality only. (a) How many points do $S_2(\emptyset)$ and $S_3(\emptyset)$ have? (b) If $M \models T$ and $A \subseteq M$ has $k$ elements, how many points does $S_1(A)$ have? (c) Which points of $S_1(M)$ are isolated?

4. ◦ In the field $\C$, decide which of these numbers lie in $\operatorname{dcl}(\set{i})$ and which lie in $\operatorname{acl}(\set{i})$, using [algebraic and definable closure](/DKAGC639W9/) in algebraically closed fields. (a) $1/(1+i)$. (b) $\sqrt{2}$. (c) $(1+i)^2$. (d) $(1+i)/\sqrt{2}$. (e) $\pi$.

5. ◦ Let $\mathcal{M} = (\Z, s)$ with $s(n) = n + 1$. Find $\operatorname{acl}(\emptyset)$, $\operatorname{dcl}(\set{0})$ and $\operatorname{acl}(\set{0})$. In the elementary extension made of two disjoint copies of $\Z$ with the successor map, what is $\operatorname{acl}(\set{0})$?

6. ◦ Which of these structures are $\aleph_0$-[saturated](/RMTZ5J64FX/)? (a) $(\R, <)$. (b) $(\Q \cap [0, 1], <)$. (c) $(\Z, <)$. (d) The field $\Q(t)^{\mathrm{alg}}$, the algebraic closure of $\Q(t)$ with $t$ transcendental. (e) $(\N, <)$.

7. ◦ Which of these structures are [rigid](/EPNZKJQHWJ/)? (a) The group $(\Z, +)$. (b) The field $\Q(\sqrt[3]{2})$. (c) The order $\omega + \omega$. (d) The field $\Q(\sqrt{2})$. (e) The order $(\Q, <)$.

8. ◦ Find the orbits of $\operatorname{Aut}(\Z, <)$ on $\Z^2$, and show that each orbit is the set of pairs of integers that realize one complete $2$-type over $\emptyset$.

9. • In $(\Z, <)$ describe the [Stone space](/VDY4PG5WHN/) $S_1(\set{0})$: list its points, say which are realized and which are isolated, and show that it is homeomorphic to $\Z \cup \set{-\infty, +\infty}$, the two-point compactification of the discrete space $\Z$.

10. • In the theory of dense linear orders without endpoints, count the points of $S_2(\set{0})$ in $(\Q, <)$, and of $S_2(\set{0, 1})$.

11. • Let $\mathcal{M}$ be a structure and $n \ge 1$. Show that a type $p \in S_n(M)$ is [isolated](/A0VNTN725W/) exactly when it is realized in $\mathcal{M}$, and that the realized types form a dense subset of $S_n(M)$. Deduce that if $M$ is infinite, then $S_1(M)$ has a point that is not realized in $\mathcal{M}$.

12. • Let $T$ be the theory of $(\Q, <, c_n)_{n \in \N}$ with $c_n = n$. (a) List the points of $S_1(\emptyset)$ and say which are isolated. (b) Is the non-isolated type realized in $(\Q, <, n)_{n \in \N}$? In $(\Q, <, 1 - 1/(n+1))_{n \in \N}$? (c) To which familiar space is $S_1(\emptyset)$ homeomorphic?

13. • Let $E$ be an equivalence relation on a countable set $M$ with exactly one class of size $n$ for each $n \ge 1$ and no other classes, and let $T = \operatorname{Th}(M, E)$. (a) Find $\operatorname{acl}(\emptyset)$ and $\operatorname{dcl}(\emptyset)$. (b) Describe $S_1(\emptyset)$ and its isolated points. (c) Describe a countable $\aleph_0$-saturated model of $T$.

14. • In the ordered group $(\R, +, <, 0)$, which has quantifier elimination as a divisible ordered abelian group, find $\operatorname{dcl}(\emptyset)$, $\operatorname{dcl}(\set{1})$ and $\operatorname{acl}(\set{1, \sqrt{2}})$, and decide whether $\sqrt{2} \in \operatorname{acl}(\set{1})$.

15. • Let $T = \operatorname{Th}(\Z, <, 0)$, and let $p_+(x) = \set{0 < x \wedge \delta_n(0, x) : n \in \N}$ and $p_-(x) = \set{x < 0 \wedge \delta_n(x, 0) : n \in \N}$, with $\delta_n$ as in Exercise 1. Show that $p_+$ and $p_-$ are not isolated, and give countable models of $T$ that realize neither of them, only $p_+$, only $p_-$, and both.

16. • Show that every countable algebraically closed field of characteristic $0$ is strongly $\aleph_0$-[homogeneous](/4NJS7W57VK/), and that it is $\aleph_0$-saturated exactly when its transcendence degree is $\aleph_0$.

17. • Show that every $\aleph_1$-saturated dense linear order has at least $2^{\aleph_0}$ elements.

18. • Show that $(\R, <)$ is $\aleph_1$-[universal](/F30ZVR4AD5/) but not universal.

19. • Let $\mathcal{M} = \omega \times \Z$, ordered lexicographically with the $\omega$ coordinate first. It is a model of $\operatorname{Th}(\Z, <)$. Show that all elements of $\mathcal{M}$ have the same type over $\emptyset$, find the orbits of $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal{M})$ on $M$, and show that $\mathcal{M}$ is not $\aleph_0$-homogeneous.

20. • In a [monster model](/EYTTQZ0E1S/) of $\mathrm{ACF}_0$ let $a$ be transcendental over $\Q$. Decide whether the two tuples have the same type over the given set. (a) $a$ and $a^2$ over $\emptyset$. (b) $(a, a^2)$ and $(a^2, a)$ over $\emptyset$. (c) $a$ and $-a$ over $\set{a^2}$. (d) $a$ and $a + 1$ over $\set{a^2}$.

21. ★ Show that every $\aleph_1$-saturated elementary extension of $(\N, <)$ has at least $2^{\aleph_0}$ elements, and that one of exactly this size exists. You may use that every model of $\operatorname{Th}(\N, <)$ is $\N + L \times \Z$ for a linear order $L$, with $L \times \Z$ ordered lexicographically.

22. ★ Let $\mathcal{M}$ be a [special model](/CBNPB0QZGQ/) of size $\lambda$. Show that $\mathcal{M}$ is $\operatorname{cf}(\lambda)$-saturated, and deduce that every special model of size $\beth_{\omega_1}$ is $\aleph_1$-saturated.

23. ★ Every countable model of $\operatorname{Th}(\Z, <)$ is $L \times \Z$ for a countable nonempty linear order $L$. Show that the following are equivalent: (i) $L \times \Z$ is $\aleph_0$-saturated; (ii) $L \times \Z$ is [recursively saturated](/ZGEXNRHC9M/); (iii) $L \cong (\Q, <)$.

24. ★ Show that an infinite structure that is $\aleph_0$-saturated and strongly $\aleph_0$-homogeneous is not rigid, and deduce that no infinite saturated structure is rigid. Give an infinite rigid structure in which every element is definable over $\emptyset$.

25. ★ Let $\mathbb{M}$ be a monster model and $A$ a small set. Show that $b \in \operatorname{dcl}(A)$ exactly when every $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{M}/A)$ fixes $b$, and that $b \in \operatorname{acl}(A)$ exactly when the orbit of $b$ under $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{M}/A)$ is finite.
