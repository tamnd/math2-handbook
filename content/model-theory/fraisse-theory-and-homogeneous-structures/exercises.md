1. ◦ For each class, say whether it has the hereditary property and whether it has the [joint embedding property](/59FQ5Q2B8R/): (a) the finite forests; (b) the finite connected graphs; (c) the linear orders with at most three elements; (d) the finite structures with one unary predicate $P$ in which at most one element satisfies $P$. For each class that is an [age](/5FKW715RGJ/), name a structure whose age it is.

2. ◦ Counting the empty graph, list the isomorphism types of graphs in $\operatorname{Age}(C_5)$, where $C_5$ is the [cycle graph](/0QQ0F12962/) on five vertices. Which of them are not in $\operatorname{Age}(C_4)$, and which members of $\operatorname{Age}(C_4)$ are not in $\operatorname{Age}(C_5)$?

3. ◦ Let $A$ be the linear order $a < a'$, let $B$ be $a < b < a'$ and let $C$ be $a < c < a'$, with the inclusions of $A$. List, up to isomorphism over $B$ and $C$, the amalgams $D$ in which every element is the image of an element of $B$ or of $C$. Which of them are strong?

4. ◦ Which of the following finite graphs are [ultrahomogeneous](/H8VM37SSAX/): $K_4$, $C_4$, the disjoint union $2K_2$ of two edges, the path $P_4$ on four vertices, the complete bipartite graph $K_{2,3}$, and the disjoint union $K_1 \sqcup K_2$? For each one that is not, give an isomorphism between one-point induced subgraphs that does not extend to an automorphism.

5. ◦ In the graph on $\N$ in which $i < j$ are adjacent when the $i$-th binary digit of $j$ is $1$, (a) list the neighbours of $5$ among $0, 1, \ldots, 10$; (b) for $U = \set{1, 3}$ and $V = \set{0, 2}$, compute $z = 2^m + \sum_{u \in U} 2^u$ with $m$ the least integer larger than every element of $U \cup V$, and find the smallest vertex adjacent to every vertex of $U$ and to no vertex of $V$.

6. ◦ For each countable graph, decide whether it is ultrahomogeneous and, if it is, where it sits on the list of the [Lachlan–Woodrow theorem](/F7PA1RVBCG/): (a) $\aleph_0 \cdot K_3$; (b) the [complement](/0MCYJ72VGA/) of $\aleph_0 \cdot K_2$; (c) $K_{\aleph_0} \sqcup K_2$; (d) the complement of the [Henson graph](/TZT25G2YB3/) $G_3$; (e) the [Rado graph](/RMXR0FEF54/) with one vertex removed.

7. ◦ With the predimension $\delta(A) = \abs{A} - \abs{E(A)}$ of [Hrushovski amalgamation](/933BFNKPXF/), find all self-sufficient subsets of the path $a - b - c - d$, and all self-sufficient subsets of the cycle $C_4$. Are both graphs in $\mathcal{K}_0$?

8. ◦ A [Ramsey class](/8XS8FCTF8T/) consists of [rigid structures](/EPNZKJQHWJ/). For each class, either give a member with a nontrivial automorphism or explain why every member is rigid: (a) finite tournaments; (b) finite Boolean algebras; (c) finite equivalence relations; (d) finite ordered graphs; (e) finite linear orders with one unary predicate.

9. • Let $\mathcal{K}$ be the class of finite graphs in which every vertex has degree at most $1$. Show that $\mathcal{K}$ has the [amalgamation property](/XSCSX39GEZ/), give an amalgamation problem in $\mathcal{K}$ with no strong amalgam, and identify the [Fraïssé limit](/HJVRBDQSGG/) of $\mathcal{K}$ on the Lachlan–Woodrow list.

10. • Show that the finite equivalence relations form a Fraïssé class, and describe its limit. Viewed as a graph, with $x \ne y$ adjacent when $x E y$, which graph on the Lachlan–Woodrow list is it?

11. • Let $\mathcal{K}$ be the class of finite bipartite graphs with a named bipartition: structures with unary predicates $P_0$ and $P_1$ that partition the vertices and edges only between $P_0$ and $P_1$. Show that $\mathcal{K}$ has free amalgamation, and state the extension property of its limit $M$. Then show that the graph obtained from $M$ by forgetting $P_0$ and $P_1$ is not ultrahomogeneous.

12. • Show that the finite tournaments have strong amalgamation. State the extension property of their limit, the random tournament, and show that it is not isomorphic to the tournament on $\Q$ in which $x \to y$ when $x < y$.

13. • Show that the complement of an ultrahomogeneous graph is ultrahomogeneous. Show that if $\mathcal{K}$ is a Fraïssé class of finite graphs, the complements of its members form a Fraïssé class whose limit is the complement of the limit of $\mathcal{K}$. Identify the limit of the finite graphs with no independent set of three vertices.

14. • In the Henson graph $G_3$, show that two non-adjacent vertices have infinitely many common neighbours and two adjacent vertices have none. Conclude that $G_3$ is not isomorphic to the Rado graph, and show that it is not isomorphic to its own complement.

15. • Count the orbits of $\operatorname{Aut}(\Q, <)$ on $\Q^2$ and on $\Q^3$, and the orbits of the automorphism group of the Rado graph $R$ on $R^2$ and on $R^3$. How many orbits does $\operatorname{Aut}(R)$ have on $n$-tuples of distinct vertices?

16. • Let $s$ be the successor function on $\Z$ and $S$ the successor relation, $S(x, y)$ when $y = x + 1$. Describe the finitely generated substructures of $(\Z, s)$ and show that $(\Z, s)$ is ultrahomogeneous. Then show that $(\Z, S)$ is not ultrahomogeneous, and describe its age.

17. • The finite fields of characteristic $p$ have the amalgamation property. Show that the amalgam of $\F_{p^2}$ with itself over $\F_p$ is never strong. Show that an element $\alpha \in \F_{p^2} \setminus \F_p$ has exactly two images under the automorphisms of the algebraic closure of $\F_p$.

18. • Let $G = \operatorname{Aut}(\Q, <)$ with the [topology of pointwise convergence](/FVGB8ZP67B/), and let $H = \set{g \in G : g(\Z) = \Z}$. Show that $H$ is not open. Then show directly, without the [small index property](/RZAF6NVEXC/), that $H$ has index $2^{\aleph_0}$ in $G$.

19. • In the [proof of EPPA](/1Q99SE2FDY/), the graph $H$ built from a graph $G$ on $n$ vertices has the vertices $(a, f)$ with $f \colon V \setminus \set{a} \to \set{0, 1}$. Show that $H$ depends only on $n$, that it is regular of degree $(n-1) 2^{n-2}$, and that it has $n(n-1) 2^{2n-4}$ edges. For $G = K_2$ with vertices $a < b$, draw $H$, give the embedding $e$, and write out the automorphism of $H$ that the proof gives for the map $a \mapsto b$.

20. ★ Let $M$ be the Fraïssé limit of $\mathcal{K}$. Show that every countable structure $N$ with $\operatorname{Age}(N) \subseteq \mathcal{K}$ embeds into $M$.

21. ★ For $k \ge 1$, let $E_k$ say that for all disjoint $U$ and $V$ with $\abs{U} + \abs{V} \le k$ there is a vertex outside $U \cup V$ adjacent to every vertex of $U$ and to none of $V$. Show that a random graph on $n$ vertices, each edge present with probability $\frac{1}{2}$, satisfies $E_k$ with probability tending to $1$ as $n \to \infty$. Deduce that the theory of the Rado graph is not finitely axiomatizable.

22. ★ Let $\mathcal{K}$ be a class of finite structures with the hereditary property, the joint embedding property and the Ramsey property, with colourings of embeddings. Show that $\mathcal{K}$ has the amalgamation property.

23. ★ With $\delta(A) = \abs{A} - \abs{E(A)}$, use submodularity to prove that if $A \le B$ and $C \subseteq B$ then $A \cap C \le C$, and that $A \le B \le C$ implies $A \le C$. Deduce that if $A_1 \le B$ and $A_2 \le B$ then $A_1 \cap A_2 \le B$, so that every $A \subseteq B$ has a smallest self-sufficient superset in $B$.

24. ★ Let $A \le B$ and $A \subseteq C$ with $B, C \in \mathcal{K}_0$, and let $D$ be the free amalgam of $B$ and $C$ over $A$. Show that $D \in \mathcal{K}_0$ and $C \le D$.
