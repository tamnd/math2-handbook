1. ◦ Decide whether each sequence $(a_n)_{n \in \N}$ is an [order-indiscernible sequence](/4016QKAHFB/) over $\emptyset$, and give a formula that separates two increasing tuples when it is not. (a) $a_n = n$ in $(\Z, +)$. (b) $a_n = -n$ in $(\Q, <)$. (c) $a_n$ the $n$-th prime number in $(\N, \cdot)$. (d) $a_n = 5$ for all $n$, in any structure. For (b), say which of $x_1 < x_2$, $x_2 < x_1$ and $\exists y \, (x_2 < y \wedge y < x_1)$ belong to the Ehrenfeucht–Mostowski type. Which of the four sequences are indiscernible sets?

2. ◦ Let $(a_i)_{i \in \Q}$ be an order-indiscernible sequence that is not constant. Decide whether each sequence is order-indiscernible. (a) $(a_{-i})_{i \in \Q}$. (b) $(a_{2n})_{n \in \N}$. (c) $(a_{\floor{n/2}})_{n \in \N}$.

3. ◦ In $(\Z, +, <)$, colour each pair $i < j$ of natural numbers by whether $j - i$ is even, that is, by the formula $\exists z \, (x_2 = x_1 + z + z)$ at $(i, j)$. Find an infinite set of natural numbers on which all pairs have the same colour. What is the largest size of a set on which every pair has an odd difference?

4. ◦ Let $T$ be a theory with infinite models, $T^*$ a Skolemization and $\Phi$ an Ehrenfeucht–Mostowski type, as in [Ehrenfeucht–Mostowski models](/TSGB29FJM0/). Give $\abs{\operatorname{EM}(I, \Phi)}$, or the best bounds part 4 gives, in each case. (a) $L$ countable and $I$ the ordinal $\omega_5$. (b) $L$ countable and $I = (\Q, <)$. (c) $\abs{L} = \aleph_3$ and $I = \omega$. (d) $\abs{L} = \aleph_3$ and $I = \omega_7$.

5. ◦ For each structure, give the pair $(\abs{M}, \abs{P^M})$ and decide whether [Vaught's two-cardinal theorem](/XT227BYP7B/) applies to its theory. (a) $(\R, +, \cdot, P)$ with $P = \Q$. (b) $(\C, +, \cdot, P)$ with $P = \R$. (c) $(\Q, <, P)$ with $P = \Z$. (d) $(\R, <, P)$ with $P$ a single point.

6. ◦ True or false? (a) Every first-order sentence with a model of size $\aleph_7$ has a model of size $\aleph_{100}$. (b) Every first-order sentence with arbitrarily large finite models has an infinite model. (c) Every sentence of $L_{\omega_1\omega}$ in a countable signature with a model of size $\beth_\omega$ has arbitrarily large models. (d) Every sentence of $L_{\omega_1\omega}$ in a countable signature with a model of size $\beth_{\omega_1}$ has arbitrarily large models. Use the [Hanf number](/A9XBRF3A7Z/).

7. ◦ (a) For which of $\kappa = \aleph_0$, $\aleph_1$ and $\aleph_\omega$ is $\kappa^{<\kappa} = \kappa$ provable in ZFC, refutable in ZFC, or dependent on the value of $2^{\aleph_0}$? (b) Assume the generalized continuum hypothesis, and let a countable theory $T$ have an $(\aleph_5, \aleph_2)$-model. Which of these does the chapter give: an $(\aleph_1, \aleph_0)$-model; an $(\aleph_{\omega + 2}, \aleph_{\omega + 1})$-model; an $(\aleph_{\omega + 1}, \aleph_\omega)$-model by [Chang's two-cardinal theorem](/4STP69KESQ/)?

8. • Prove that an infinite order-indiscernible sequence is either constant or injective. Then show that a family of at least two distinct elements of a linear order $(M, <)$ is never an indiscernible set over $\emptyset$.

9. • Show that no injective sequence $(a_n)_{n \in \N}$ in $(\Z, <)$ is order-indiscernible. Then show that in the model $\Q \times \Z$ of $\operatorname{Th}(\Z, <)$, ordered lexicographically, the sequence $a_n = (n, 0)$ is order-indiscernible.

10. • Let $L$ be countable and let $T$ have an infinite model. Use [Ehrenfeucht–Mostowski models](/TSGB29FJM0/) to show that $T$ has a countable model with exactly $2^{\aleph_0}$ automorphisms.

11. • Let $T$ be the theory of an infinite set with a unary predicate $P$ such that $P$ and its complement are both infinite; take as known that $T$ has quantifier elimination. (a) For which cardinals $\kappa$ and $\lambda$ does $T$ have a $(\kappa, \lambda)$-model? (b) Give a Vaughtian pair of countable models of $T$. (c) How many models of size $\aleph_1$ does $T$ have, up to isomorphism?

12. • The theory of infinite vector spaces over $\Q$ is strongly minimal: in every model, each definable subset of the universe, with parameters, is finite or cofinite. Show that for no formula $\varphi(x)$ does this theory have a model $M$ with $\varphi(M)$ infinite and $\abs{\varphi(M)} < \abs{M}$. Contrast this with Exercise 11.

13. • Let $\sigma$ be the sentence of $L_{\omega_1\omega}$, in the signature $\set{<, c_0, c_1, \ldots}$, saying that $<$ is a linear order and that $\forall x \, \forall y \, \bigl(x < y \to \bigvee_{n} (x < c_n \wedge c_n < y)\bigr)$. Show that every model of $\sigma$ has size at most $2^{\aleph_0}$ and that $\sigma$ has a model of size $2^{\aleph_0}$. Deduce that the Hanf number of $L_{\omega_1\omega}$ is greater than $2^{\aleph_0}$.

14. • Let $(a_n)_{n \in \N}$ be distinct vertices of the [Rado graph](/RMXR0FEF54/) with no edges between them. Show that $(a_n)$ is an indiscernible set, using that the Rado graph is [ultrahomogeneous](/H8VM37SSAX/). Then show that every injective indiscernible set of vertices is either a set of pairwise adjacent vertices or a set of pairwise non-adjacent ones.

15. • In the Rado graph, find vertices $b_n, c_n$ for $n \in \N$ such that $b_i$ is adjacent to $c_j$ exactly when $i < j$, with no other edges among them. Show that the sequence of pairs $(b_n, c_n)$ is order-indiscernible but not an indiscernible set.

16. • Let $T$ be a countable complete theory with a $(\kappa, \lambda)$-model for some $\kappa > \lambda \ge \aleph_0$. Show that $T$ also has an $(\aleph_1, \aleph_1)$-model, and deduce that $T$ is not $\aleph_1$-categorical.

17. • Let $(a_n)_{n \in \N}$ be an injective order-indiscernible sequence and $m \ge 1$. Show that $(a_n)_{n \ge m}$ is order-indiscernible over $\set{a_0, \dots, a_{m-1}}$, and that $(a_n)_{n \in \N}$ is not order-indiscernible over $\set{a_0}$.

18. ★ Prove the Ramsey form of the [existence of indiscernibles](/RG6D2FAS14/): if $\mathcal{M}$ has an injective sequence $(b_n)_{n \in \N}$ and $I$ is an infinite linear order, then some $\mathcal{N} \equiv \mathcal{M}$ has an injective indiscernible sequence $(a_i)_{i \in I}$ such that every formula true at all increasing tuples of $(b_n)$ is true at all increasing tuples of $(a_i)$.

19. ★ Let $L$ be countable and let $T$ have a $(\kappa, \lambda)$-model with $\kappa > \lambda \ge \aleph_0$. Show that $T$ has a Vaughtian pair $\mathcal{N}_0 \prec \mathcal{M}_0$ of countable models. Use the Löwenheim–Skolem theorem twice, the second time for the structure $(\mathcal{M}, N)$ with a new predicate for $N$.

20. ★ Say that a formula $\varphi(\bar x; \bar y)$ has the order property in $T$ if some model has tuples $\bar a_n, \bar b_n$ for $n \in \N$ with $\models \varphi(\bar a_n, \bar b_m)$ exactly when $n < m$, and call $T$ stable if no formula has it. Prove that in a stable theory every order-indiscernible sequence indexed by $\Q$ is an indiscernible set.

21. ★ Prove part 2 of the theorem on [Ehrenfeucht–Mostowski models](/TSGB29FJM0/): every order embedding $f : I \to J$ extends to a unique elementary embedding $\hat f : \operatorname{EM}(I, \Phi) \to \operatorname{EM}(J, \Phi)$ with $\hat f(a_i) = a_{f(i)}$.
