1. ◦ Compute the [quantifier rank](/FVEVRE5XNZ/) of each formula. (a) $\forall x\, \exists y\, (x < y \wedge \exists z\, (z < x))$. (b) $\exists x\, P(x) \vee \forall y\, \forall z\, (y = z)$. (c) $\forall x\, (\exists y\, R(x, y) \to \exists y\, R(y, x))$. (d) $\neg\neg \exists x\, \exists y\, \exists z\, (x < y \wedge y < z)$. (e) $\exists x\, (\exists y\, R(x, y) \wedge \exists y\, R(y, x) \wedge \exists y\, R(y, y))$.

2. ◦ Write a sentence of quantifier rank $2$ that is true in $(\N, <)$ and false in $(\Z, <)$.

3. ◦ Let $A$ and $B$ be sets with no structure, with $\abs{A} = 4$ and $\abs{B} = 6$. What is the largest $n$ for which Duplicator wins the [Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé game](/2RE7YAZK8R/) $G_n(A, B)$? Describe how Spoiler wins the game with one more round.

4. ◦ For each pair of finite linear orders, give the largest $n$ with $L_m \equiv_n L_k$, where $L_m$ has $m$ elements. (a) $L_3$ and $L_4$. (b) $L_5$ and $L_6$. (c) $L_{15}$ and $L_{20}$. (d) $L_{31}$ and $L_{100}$.

5. ◦ Which pairs are partially isomorphic, in the sense of a [back-and-forth system](/EWB8KXZMCV/)? (a) $(\Q, <)$ and $(\Q \cap (0, 1), <)$. (b) $(\Q, <)$ and $(\Q \cap [0, 1], <)$. (c) $(\R, <)$ and $(\R \setminus \set{0}, <)$. (d) $(\Z, <)$ and $(\N, <)$.

6. ◦ Give a winning strategy for Spoiler in $G_2((\N, <), (\Z, <))$.

7. ◦ Let $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{B}$ be countable structures in the same relational signature. Which statements are always true? (a) If $\mathcal{A} \cong_p \mathcal{B}$, then $\mathcal{A} \cong \mathcal{B}$. (b) If $\mathcal{A} \equiv \mathcal{B}$, then $\mathcal{A} \cong_p \mathcal{B}$. (c) If $\mathcal{A} \equiv_{\infty\omega} \mathcal{B}$, then $\mathcal{A} \equiv \mathcal{B}$.

8. • Spoiler can win $G_3(L_7, L_6)$. Find the only element he can pick in $L_7$ in the first round that leads to a win, and show that every answer of Duplicator in $L_6$ then loses the last two rounds.

9. • Show that "the number of elements is divisible by $3$" is not expressed by any first-order sentence on finite linear orders. Name the pairs of orders you use.

10. • A structure $(A, P)$ consists of a finite set and one unary relation. Show that the property "$P$ and its complement have the same number of elements" is not first-order, using the structures with $n$ elements in $P$ and $n$ outside, and with $n$ elements in $P$ and $n + 1$ outside.

11. • A graph is regular if all its vertices have the same degree. Duplicator wins $G_n(K_{n,n}, K_{n,n+1})$, as in [non-definability by games](/VT2PSXKM50/). Deduce that regularity is not first-order on finite graphs. Does the same family show that "the number of edges is even" is not first-order?

12. • What is the least quantifier rank of a sentence that expresses "at least $10$ elements" (a) on finite linear orders, (b) on finite sets with no structure? Justify both answers with [games on finite linear orders](/ZZPXVVZARC/) and the result for sets.

13. • Let $D$ be the set of rationals of the form $k/2^j$, and $T$ the set of rationals of the form $k/3^j$, with $k \in \Z$ and $j \in \N$. Are the structures $(\Q, <, D)$ and $(\Q, <, T)$, with one order and one unary relation, isomorphic? Prove your answer with the [back-and-forth method](/6F2DB4HTJR/).

14. • Let $\R + \R$ be the order formed by two copies of $(\R, <)$, the first below the second. Show that $(\R, <) \cong_p (\R + \R, <)$, but that the two orders are not isomorphic, although they have the same cardinality.

15. • Write a sentence of $L_{\omega_1 \omega}$ whose models are exactly the linear orders isomorphic to $(\N, <)$. Explain how this fits with [Karp's theorem](/KAWWZ2DF0G/).

16. • Let $\omega \cdot 2$ be the order formed by two copies of $(\N, <)$, the first below the second. Find the least $n$ such that some sentence of rank $n$ is true in $\omega \cdot 2$ and false in $(\N, <)$, and give such a sentence.

17. ★ Let $\omega + \zeta$ be the order formed by $(\N, <)$ followed by a copy of $(\Z, <)$. Show that Duplicator wins $G_n((\N, <), \omega + \zeta)$ for every $n$. Conclude that the two orders are elementarily equivalent but not partially isomorphic.

18. ★ Let $C_k$ be the cycle with $k$ vertices. Show that Duplicator wins $G_n(C_{2m}, C_m \sqcup C_m)$ whenever $m \ge 2^{n+1}$, and deduce that connectivity is not first-order on finite graphs.

19. ★ For finite structures $(A, P)$ and $(B, Q)$ with one unary relation each, show that $(A, P) \equiv_n (B, Q)$ exactly when $\abs{P}$ and $\abs{Q}$ are equal or both at least $n$, and $\abs{A \setminus P}$ and $\abs{B \setminus Q}$ are equal or both at least $n$.

20. ★ Show that if $\mathcal{A} \equiv_n \mathcal{A}'$ and $\mathcal{B} \equiv_n \mathcal{B}'$ are linear orders, then $\mathcal{A} + \mathcal{B} \equiv_n \mathcal{A}' + \mathcal{B}'$, where $+$ puts the second order above the first. Deduce that $(\Z, <) + L_{63} \equiv_6 (\Z, <) + L_{64}$.
