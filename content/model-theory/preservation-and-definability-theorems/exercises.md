1. ◦ For each sentence, say which of the forms universal, $\forall\exists$, positive and Horn it has as written (a $\forall\exists$ sentence may have an empty block of quantifiers): (a) $\forall x \forall y \, (E(x, y) \to E(y, x))$; (b) $\forall x \, \exists y \, (x < y)$; (c) $\exists x \, \forall y \, (x \le y)$; (d) $\forall x \forall y \, (x \le y \lor y \le x)$; (e) $\exists x \, \neg(x = e)$.

2. ◦ Which of these classes are closed under [substructures](/676H7EGTKJ/)? Give a substructure that leaves the class where the answer is no. (a) Divisible abelian groups in $\set{+, -, 0}$. (b) Ordered fields in $\set{+, -, \cdot, 0, 1, <}$. (c) Graphs in which every vertex has at most three neighbours. (d) Linear orders without endpoints.

3. ◦ Which of these classes are closed under unions of chains? (a) Dense linear orders without endpoints. (b) Dense linear orders with endpoints. (c) Abelian groups in which every element is divisible by $2$. (d) Graphs without isolated vertices. For each answer, say what the [Chang–Łoś–Suszko theorem](/RK7NK2P75J/) gives.

4. ◦ Let $E$ be a binary relation symbol. Which of these properties are preserved by surjective homomorphisms: (a) $E$ is reflexive; (b) some pair is in $E$; (c) $E$ is symmetric; (d) $E$ is transitive; (e) the domain has at least two elements? Give a counterexample for each answer no.

5. ◦ Which of these classes are closed under direct products of two structures: (a) abelian groups; (b) integral domains; (c) groups in which $x^2 = e$ for all $x$; (d) fields of characteristic $3$? Which of them are [universal Horn classes](/9A4CYWXPEA/)?

6. ◦ (a) Let $T$ be the group axioms in the signature $\set{\cdot, e}$ together with $\forall x \, (x \cdot f(x) = e)$, for a new unary function symbol $f$. Show that $T$ defines $f$ implicitly, and give an explicit definition of the graph of $f$. (b) Use Padoa's method to show that no formula of the ring signature without parameters defines the set $\set{i}$ in the field $\C$.

7. ◦ Let $f$ be a unary function symbol. Show that the reducts to the empty signature of the models of $\forall x \, (f(x) \ne x \land f(f(x)) = x)$ are the sets that are infinite or finite of even size. Deduce that this class is a $\mathrm{PC}$ class, and show that the class of finite sets of even size is not a $\mathrm{PC}_\Delta$ class.

8. • Let $\mathcal{K}$ be the class of linear orders in which every element has an immediate successor. Show that $\mathcal{K}$ is elementary, that it is not a [universal class](/ZSTEZ3PRT9/), and that it is not axiomatized by $\forall\exists$ sentences.

9. • Write a universal sentence that axiomatizes, together with the graph axioms, the graphs in which every vertex has at most two neighbours. Find the graphs on four vertices that are minimal forbidden induced subgraphs for this class, up to isomorphism.

10. • Show that the [forests](/0GGXWDNKBR/) form a universal class of graphs, that this class is not finitely axiomatizable, and that it is not a universal Horn class.

11. • The example of the $k$-colourable graphs in [universal classes](/ZSTEZ3PRT9/) uses axioms that say "$F$ is not a subgraph", with a conjunct saying that the vertices are distinct. Show that the conjunct can be dropped, so that the $k$-colourable graphs form a universal Horn class. Check directly that the class is closed under direct products of nonempty families.

12. • Show that the class of nontrivial groups, in the signature $\set{\cdot, {}^{-1}, e}$, is closed under reduced products. Show that it is not a universal Horn class.

13. • Let $T = \mathrm{Th}(\R, +, 0, \cdot)$. Use Padoa's method, applied to the graph of multiplication, to show that multiplication is not definable from $+$ and $0$ modulo $T$.

14. • Show that the set $\Z$ is not definable in $(\Q, <)$, first without parameters by an automorphism, and then with parameters, using the form of the definable sets of dense linear orders.

15. • Let $P_1 = \set{q \in \Q : 0 < q < 1}$ and $P_2 = \set{q \in \Q : 0 < q < \pi}$. The structures $(\Q, <, P_1)$ and $(\Q, <, P_2)$ are countable and $\aleph_0$-categorical, hence saturated. Compute the number of images of $P_j$ under the automorphisms of $(\Q, <)$ for $j = 1, 2$, and say what the [Chang–Makkai theorem](/MFR9Z5PPH4/) gives in each case.

16. • Let $T$ be the theory of dense linear orders without endpoints. Show that the models of $T_\forall$ are exactly the nonempty linear orders.

17. • A commutative ring is von Neumann regular if $\forall x \, \exists y \, (x \cdot x \cdot y = x)$. Show that every [reduced product](/F4WRD396PB/) of fields is a commutative von Neumann regular ring, and that $\R^{\N} / F$, for $F$ the filter of cofinite subsets of $\N$, is not a field.

18. ★ Show that the [bi-orderable groups](/9BM25E20JQ/) form a universal class in the signature $\set{\cdot, {}^{-1}, e}$.

19. ★ Show that the class of [connected graphs](/0KWFFMDF8T/) is not a $\mathrm{PC}_\Delta$ class.

20. ★ Show that the class of graphs that are not [bipartite](/0ZVQKTRKVQ/) is closed under homomorphisms of graphs but is not an elementary class. Explain why this does not contradict [Lyndon's theorem](/8DJSRWME2X/).

21. ★ Let $\varphi$ be a sentence preserved by all homomorphisms, not only surjective ones. Show that $\varphi$ is logically equivalent to an existential positive sentence.
