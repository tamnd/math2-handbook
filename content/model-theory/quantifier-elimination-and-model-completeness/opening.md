A theory $T$ has [quantifier elimination](/7FDDXV3JK7/) when every formula is equivalent, modulo $T$, to a formula without quantifiers. Then the [definable sets](/CY1TXHF6KQ/) of every model are Boolean combinations of the sets cut out by atomic formulas, and the theory of a structure can often be read off from the small substructure generated by its constants. In the theory of algebraically closed fields, $\exists x \, (a x^2 + b x + c = 0)$ is equivalent to $a \ne 0 \lor b \ne 0 \lor c = 0$. In general, the definable sets of $\C$ are the constructible sets, so [Chevalley's theorem](/TGN01264C4/) that projections of constructible sets are constructible is a form of the same fact. Tarski proved [quantifier elimination for real closed fields](/FSJ9NGRRPA/) with the order in the language; its geometric form is the [Tarski–Seidenberg theorem](/3W5JGDZAN0/) that projections of semialgebraic sets are semialgebraic. [Presburger arithmetic](/4W0E9G7PPV/) has quantifier elimination once divisibility predicates are added, and that is how its decidability is proved.

Abraham Robinson introduced a weaker property with algebraic content. A theory is [model complete](/YR6HCMABTE/) when every embedding between two of its models is elementary; equivalently, every formula is equivalent to an existential one. The real closed fields in the language of rings, without the order, form a model complete theory without quantifier elimination. A model companion of $T$ is a model complete theory with the same universal consequences: algebraically closed fields for fields, real closed fields for ordered fields, while the theory of groups has none. When the axioms of $T$ have the form $\forall\exists$, as here, the models of its model companion are the existentially closed models of $T$, those in which every existential statement with parameters that holds in some extension satisfying $T$ already holds. Since the existentially closed fields are the algebraically closed ones, [Hilbert's Nullstellensatz](/D85VZWS9QK/) follows, and Robinson used model completeness of real closed fields to give a new proof of Artin's solution of Hilbert's seventeenth problem.
