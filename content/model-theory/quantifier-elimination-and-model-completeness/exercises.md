1. ◦ In the theory of [algebraically closed fields](/2NBBJADY5N/), find a quantifier-free formula in $b$ and $c$ equivalent to $\exists x \, (x^2 + b x + c = 0 \land x \ne 0)$.

2. ◦ In the theory of dense linear orders without endpoints, find quantifier-free equivalents of (a) $\exists y \, (y < x \land y < z)$, (b) $\exists y \, (x < y \land y < z \land y \ne w)$, (c) $\exists y \, (x < y \land w < y \land y < z)$.

3. ◦ Explain why a consistent theory with [quantifier elimination](/7FDDXV3JK7/) in a signature without constant symbols is complete. Then decide which of these theories in the empty signature have quantifier elimination: (a) the theory of infinite sets, (b) the theory of sets with at least two elements, (c) the theory of sets with exactly five elements.

4. ◦ Show that $\mathrm{Th}(\N, <)$ is not [model complete](/YR6HCMABTE/), using the substructure $\N \setminus \set{0}$ of $(\N, <)$.

5. ◦ Let $\R_{\mathrm{alg}}$ be the field of real algebraic numbers. Is the inclusion $\R_{\mathrm{alg}} \subseteq \R$ of rings elementary? Is the inclusion $\Q \subseteq \R$? Give a reason for each answer.

6. ◦ Show that $\R_{\mathrm{alg}}$ is not an [existentially closed](/6M98MCW575/) field, by giving an existential formula with parameters in $\R_{\mathrm{alg}}$ that fails there and holds in an extension field. Is $\R_{\mathrm{alg}}$, with its order, an existentially closed ordered field?

7. ◦ Which of these theories are [inductive](/G8TRE2KSRV/), that is, preserved under unions of chains? (a) The theory of groups. (b) The theory of fields of characteristic $0$. (c) The theory of linear orders with a least element. (d) The theory of graphs in which every vertex has a neighbour.

8. ◦ Give the [cylindrical algebraic decomposition](/GE8FCGKPTR/) of $\R$ adapted to $p(x) = x^3 - x$: list its cells in order and the sign of $p$ on each.

9. • In the theory of algebraically closed fields, find a quantifier-free formula equivalent to $\exists x \, (x^2 = a \land x^3 = b)$, and prove the equivalence.

10. • In the theory of real closed fields, find a quantifier-free formula equivalent to $\exists x \, (x > 0 \land x^2 + b x + c = 0)$.

11. • Let $T$ be the theory of an equivalence relation $E$ with infinitely many classes, all of them infinite. Show that $T$ has quantifier elimination, using the extension test of the [tests for quantifier elimination](/Q1CGE4DWXQ/).

12. • Let $T = \mathrm{Th}(\Z, 0, 1, +, -, <)$, with no divisibility predicates. Show that $T$ is model complete but does not have quantifier elimination. Use [quantifier elimination for $\Z$-groups](/VKC9CR37FT/) and [Robinson's test](/QHEADWYQ64/).

13. • Let $T$ be model complete, and suppose that some model $M_0$ of $T$ embeds into every model of $T$. Show that $T$ is complete. Apply this to $\mathrm{ACF}_0$ and to the theory of real closed fields in the ring signature, naming $M_0$ in each case.

14. • Show that the theory of the [Rado graph](/RMXR0FEF54/), axiomatized by the graph axioms and the extension axioms, is the model completion of the theory of graphs. Describe the existentially closed graphs.

15. • Show that the theory of integral domains and the theory of fields have the same [model companion](/68GG7QKN7C/), and name it.

16. • In the theory of $\Z$-groups, find quantifier-free equivalents of (a) $\exists y \, (2y < x \land x < 2y + 2)$, (b) $\exists y \, (x < 3y \land 3y < x + 3)$, (c) $\exists y \, (x = 3y + 1 \land 0 < y)$.

17. • Let $T$ be the theory of dense linear orders with a least and a greatest element and at least two elements, in the signature $\set{<}$. Show that $T$ does not have quantifier elimination. Show that it does once constants $0$ and $1$ for the endpoints are added.

18. • Give the cylindrical algebraic decomposition of $\R^2$ adapted to $y^2 - x$, projecting onto the $x$-axis. How many cells does it have, and of which dimensions?

19. ★ Let $T$ be the theory of nontrivial divisible ordered abelian groups in $\set{+, -, 0, <}$. Prove that $T$ has quantifier elimination. Deduce that every subset of $\Q$ definable in $(\Q, +, -, 0, <)$ with parameters is a finite union of points and open intervals with endpoints in $\Q \cup \set{\pm\infty}$.

20. ★ Prove that the theory of atomless Boolean algebras with $0 \ne 1$, in the signature $\set{\land, \lor, \neg, 0, 1}$, has quantifier elimination. Use the extension test, and describe how a new element $b$ sits over the atoms of a finite subalgebra.

21. ★ Let $T$ be a universal theory with a model completion $T^*$. Prove that $T$ has the [amalgamation property](/XSCSX39GEZ/): if $A \subseteq B$ and $A \subseteq C$ are models of $T$, then $B$ and $C$ embed over $A$ into a common model of $T$. Then show that the hypothesis that $T$ is universal cannot be dropped, by finding a model complete theory, which is its own model completion, whose universal part lacks amalgamation.

22. ★ Use the model completeness of $\mathrm{ACF}$ to prove the [weak Nullstellensatz](/ZWS0YWGF17/) in this form: if $K$ is a field with algebraic closure $\bar K$ and $f_1, \ldots, f_m \in K[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$ generate a proper ideal, then they have a common zero in $\bar K^n$.
