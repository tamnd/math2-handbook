1. ◦ In a field, the unordered pair $\set{a, b}$ is coded by $(a + b, ab)$. (a) Give the code of $\set{2, -5}$. (b) Which pair has the code $(0, -4)$? (c) In $\C$, which pair has the code $(1, 1)$, and does this code belong to any pair in $\R$? (d) A finite set of points of the plane is coded by the coefficients of $\prod_j (Y + a_j X_1 + b_j X_2)$, listed in the order of the monomials $X_1 Y, X_2 Y, X_1^2, X_1 X_2, X_2^2$. Compute the codes of $\set{(1, 4), (3, 2)}$ and of $\set{(0, 1), (1, 0)}$.

2. ◦ Write the domain formula, the equivalence formula and the formula for the graph of addition of a $1$-dimensional $\emptyset$-[interpretation](/7R0SJPH37J/) of the ring $\Z/5\Z$ in the ring $(\Z, +, \cdot, 0, 1)$ in which $\varepsilon$ is not equality. Then show that $\Z/5\Z$ is also definable in $\Z$ without parameters, with $\varepsilon$ equality.

3. ◦ The field $\Q(\sqrt 2)$ is $2$-dimensionally definable in $\Q$, as pairs $(a, b)$ standing for $a + b\sqrt 2$. (a) Write the formula for the graph of multiplication. (b) The field $\Q$ is $2$-dimensionally interpretable in $\Z$. What is the dimension of the composite interpretation of $\Q(\sqrt 2)$ in $\Z$? (c) What is the dimension of a composite of interpretations of dimensions $2$, $3$ and $2$?

4. ◦ In the ordered field $\R$, decide for each formula whether it defines an equivalence relation on $\R$ without parameters, and when it does, give a real element that codes the class of $a$. (a) $x^2 = y^2$. (b) $x$ and $y$ have the same sign, with $0$ in a class of its own. (c) $\abs{x - y} < 1$. For (d), explain why "$x - y \in \Q$" is not given by any formula of the ordered field. Use [quantifier elimination for real closed fields](/FSJ9NGRRPA/).

5. ◦ Decide whether $D$ is [stably embedded](/14WPPDDK8M/), with a reason. (a) $D$ is a finite $\emptyset$-definable set. (b) $D$ is the whole home sort of $M$, inside $M^{\mathrm{eq}}$. (c) $D$ is a dense and codense predicate $P$ in a model of the theory of dense linear orders with such a predicate.

6. ◦ True or false? (a) A theory with [elimination of imaginaries](/6M2R0ER3KV/) has weak elimination of imaginaries. (b) The theory of an infinite set with no structure has elimination of imaginaries. (c) $\mathrm{ACF}_0$ has uniform elimination of imaginaries. (d) The theory of real closed fields has elimination of imaginaries. (e) In a theory with elimination of imaginaries, every definable set $X$ has a real tuple $\bar b$ such that the automorphisms of the monster model fixing $X$ setwise are exactly those fixing $\bar b$.

7. ◦ Let $\Gamma$ be a $\emptyset$-interpretation of $N$ in $M$, both in finite signatures, with $M$ infinite. Which statements follow? (a) If $\operatorname{Th}(M)$ is decidable, so is $\operatorname{Th}(N)$. (b) If $\operatorname{Th}(N)$ is decidable, so is $\operatorname{Th}(M)$. (c) If $\operatorname{Th}(M)$ is stable, so is $\operatorname{Th}(N)$. (d) If $\operatorname{Th}(N)$ is stable, so is $\operatorname{Th}(M)$. (e) $\abs{N} \le \abs{M}$. Give a counterexample for each statement that fails.

8. • Write a $2$-dimensional definition, without parameters, of the ring of Gaussian integers $\Z[i]$ in the ring $(\Z, +, \cdot, 0, 1)$, with formulas for the graphs of addition and multiplication. A student concludes that $\operatorname{Th}(\Z[i])$ is undecidable, since the theory of $\Z$ is undecidable. Explain what is wrong with the argument.

9. • Let $M$ be a countable structure in a countable signature with $\operatorname{Th}(M)$ [$\omega$-categorical](/A22K1X8GKD/), and let $N$ be an infinite structure in a countable signature that is $\emptyset$-interpretable in $M$. Show that $\operatorname{Th}(N)$ is $\omega$-categorical. Use the orbit form of the [Ryll-Nardzewski theorem](/1513J40TKA/) and the map $\operatorname{Aut}(M) \to \operatorname{Aut}(N)$.

10. • Take as known that $\operatorname{Th}(\Z, +)$ is stable, as is the theory of every module. Show that no infinite linear order $(L, <)$ is interpretable in $(\Z, +)$, even with parameters. Deduce that $(\Z, +, <)$ is not interpretable in $(\Z, +)$, and explain why the transfer of decidability cannot show this.

11. • Show that $(\Z, <)$ and $(\Q, <)$ are not $\emptyset$-bi-interpretable, in two ways: by comparing their automorphism groups, and by Exercise 9.

12. • Let $K$ be a field and let $E$ be the $\emptyset$-definable equivalence relation on $K^2$ given by $\set{x_1, x_2} = \set{y_1, y_2}$. (a) Show that $f(x_1, x_2) = (x_1 + x_2, x_1 x_2)$ satisfies $E(\bar x, \bar y) \leftrightarrow f(\bar x) = f(\bar y)$. (b) For $K$ algebraically closed, show that $f$ maps $K^2$ onto $K^2$, so that the sort $K^2/E$ is in $\emptyset$-definable bijection with $K^2$. (c) For $K = \R$, describe the image of $f$.

13. • In the real field, let $\varphi(x, y; u, v, w)$ be $x^2 + y^2 + ux + vy + w = 0$. (a) Which parameter tuples define the same set as $(0, 0, -1)$? (b) Describe all classes of the relation "$(u, v, w)$ and $(u', v', w')$ define the same set". (c) Give a $\emptyset$-definable map $g : \R^3 \to \R^3$ with $g(\bar c) = g(\bar c')$ exactly when $\bar c$ and $\bar c'$ define the same set. This is a variation on the canonical parameter of a line.

14. • Let $T$ be the theory of an equivalence relation $E$ with exactly two classes, both infinite. Show that the class $e = a/E$ of an element $a$ is not interdefinable with any real tuple, and that $T$ does not even have weak elimination of imaginaries. Compare with the case of infinitely many classes.

15. • Let $T$ be the theory of an infinite set with a unary predicate $P$ such that $P$ and its complement are both infinite. Take as known that $T$ has quantifier elimination. Show that $P$ is stably embedded, and that the induced structure on $P$ is a pure set.

16. • Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field and let $n \ge 1$ be prime to the characteristic of $K$. Show that the quotient of the multiplicative group $K^\times$ by the subgroup $\mu_n$ of $n$-th roots of unity is in $\emptyset$-definable bijection with $K^\times$, by an explicit map. How many elements does each class have?

17. • In a model of $\operatorname{Th}(\Z, +)$, let $E_n(x, y)$ be $\exists z \, (x - y = n! \, z)$, written with repeated addition, and let $E = \bigcap_{n \ge 1} E_n$. Show that $E$ is a $\emptyset$-type-definable equivalence relation, that it has at most $2^{\aleph_0}$ classes in every model, and that no single formula defines it. So its classes are [hyperimaginaries](/NQPCDVV3NB/) that are not imaginaries.

18. • Let $T$ be complete with elimination of imaginaries, and let $A$ be a small set of parameters in its monster model. Show that the theory $T_A$, with names for the elements of $A$, also eliminates imaginaries.

19. ★ Prove that $M^{\mathrm{eq}}$ is conservative over $M$: every subset of $M^n$ that is $\emptyset$-definable in $M^{\mathrm{eq}}$ is already $\emptyset$-definable in $M$. Work by induction on formulas of $M^{\mathrm{eq}}$ whose free variables are of the home sort, after rewriting quantifiers over the sort $M^k/E$ as quantifiers over $M^k$.

20. ★ Let $T$ be complete with elimination of imaginaries, and suppose that $T$ has two distinct $\emptyset$-definable elements. Prove that $T$ has uniform elimination of imaginaries.

21. ★ Let $K$ be a field and $k, n \ge 1$. Prove that every set $S$ of $k$ points of $K^n$ is coded by the coefficients of the polynomial $\prod_{\bar a \in S} (Y + a_1 X_1 + \dots + a_n X_n)$: the coefficients are $\emptyset$-definable from $S$, and $S$ is $\emptyset$-definable from them, uniformly in $S$.

22. ★ Let $M$ and $N$ be $\emptyset$-bi-interpretable. Prove that the induced maps $\operatorname{Aut}(M) \to \operatorname{Aut}(N)$ and $\operatorname{Aut}(N) \to \operatorname{Aut}(M)$ are inverse isomorphisms, and that both are continuous for the topology of pointwise convergence. This is the easy direction of the [reconstruction theorem](/AM0523XJ9D/).
