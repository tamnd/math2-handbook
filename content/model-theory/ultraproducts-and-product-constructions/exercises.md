1. ◦ Let $F$ be the [Fréchet filter](/QN98V7NMCD/) of cofinite subsets of $\N$, and work in the [reduced power](/F4WRD396PB/) $\Z^{\N}/F$. Decide whether $[a] = [b]$ in each case. (a) $a(n) = n \bmod 2$, and $b(n) = n \bmod 2$ for $n \ge 10$ but $b(n) = 7$ for $n < 10$. (b) $a = (0, 1, 0, 1, \ldots)$ and $b = 0$. (c) $a(n) = \max(0, 5 - n)$ and $b = 0$. (d) $a(n) = n^2$ and $b(n) = 3n - 2$.

2. ◦ Let $I = \set{1, 2, 3, 4}$ and let $F = \set{X \subseteq I : \set{2, 3} \subseteq X}$. Take the rings $\Z/2\Z$, $\Z/3\Z$, $\Z/5\Z$, $\Z/7\Z$ as the factors with indices $1, 2, 3, 4$. How many elements does the reduced product have, and which familiar ring is it?

3. ◦ Let $U$ be a nonprincipal ultrafilter on $\N$ and let $d$ be the diagonal embedding of $(\N, <)$ into the [ultrapower](/5ZVJK0V0GF/) $(\N, <)^{\N}/U$. Which statements are true? (a) $[(0, 1, 2, \ldots)] > d(m)$ for every $m \in \N$. (b) $[(0, 2, 4, \ldots)] > [(0, 1, 2, \ldots)]$. (c) $[(n \bmod 2)_n]$ is equal to $d(0)$ or to $d(1)$. (d) Some element lies strictly between $d(3)$ and $d(4)$.

4. ◦ In the [hyperreals](/421QMTFG9T/) $\R^{\N}/U$, with $U$ nonprincipal, classify each element as infinitesimal, infinite or neither, and give its sign when the sign does not depend on $U$. (a) $a = [((-1)^n/(n+1))_n]$. (b) $b = [(n^2 - 100n)_n]$. (c) $c = [(1 + 1/(n+1))_n]$.

5. ◦ Show that every ultrafilter on a finite set $I$ is principal. What is then every ultraproduct indexed by $I$?

6. ◦ Let $U$ be a nonprincipal ultrafilter on $\N$. Give the cardinality of each structure. (a) $(\Z/5\Z)^{\N}/U$. (b) $\prod_{n \ge 1} (\Z/n\Z)/U$. (c) $\Q^{\N}/U$. (d) $\prod_{n \in \N} \set{0, 1, \ldots, 7}/U$.

7. ◦ Do $(\Z, <)$ and $(\Q, <)$ have isomorphic ultrapowers? Do $(\Q, <)$ and $(\R, <)$? Use the [Keisler–Shelah theorem](/A37HPCKCE2/) and say which direction of it you need in each case.

8. • Which of these sentences are preserved by all reduced products? For each one that is not, give structures in which it holds and a reduced product of them in which it fails. (a) $\forall x\, \forall y\, (xy = yx)$. (b) $\forall x\, (x \neq 0 \to \exists y\, (xy = 1))$. (c) $\forall x\, \forall y\, (x \le y \vee y \le x)$. (d) $\forall x\, \forall y\, \forall z\, (x \le y \wedge y \le z \to x \le z)$. (e) $\forall x\, (x \cdot x = 1 \to x = 1)$.

9. • Let $U$ be a nonprincipal ultrafilter on the set of primes and let $G = \prod_p (\Z/p\Z)/U$, an ultraproduct of groups under addition. Show that $G$ is torsion-free and divisible. What is $G$ as a vector space over $\Q$, and what is its dimension?

10. • Show that the class of torsion-free groups is elementary but not basic elementary. Use the [characterization of elementary classes](/4HNAJQQ8Z0/) and the previous exercise.

11. • Let $U$ be a nonprincipal ultrafilter on the set of primes and $K = \prod_p \F_p/U$. (a) Show that, whatever $U$ is, at least one of $-1$, $2$, $-2$ is a square in $K$. (b) Find a set $S$ of primes such that $3$ is a square in $K$ exactly when $S \in U$.

12. • For $n \ge 1$ let $L_n = \set{0, 1, \ldots, n-1}$ with its usual order, and let $L = \prod_{n \ge 1} L_n/U$ for a nonprincipal ultrafilter $U$ on the positive integers. Decide: (a) does $L$ have a least and a greatest element; (b) does every element except the greatest have an immediate successor; (c) does some element have infinitely many elements below it; (d) is $L$ well ordered?

13. • Let $P_n$ be the path graph with vertices $0, 1, \ldots, n$ and edges between $i$ and $i+1$. Using an ultraproduct of the $P_n$, show that the class of connected graphs is not elementary.

14. • Decide which pairs of abelian groups are elementarily equivalent. (a) $\Z$ and $\Z \times \Z$. (b) $\Z \times \Q$ and $\Z \times \Q \times \Q$. (c) $\Q$ and $\Q \times \Q$. (d) $\Z/2\Z \times \Z/4\Z$ and $\Z/8\Z$. For (b), say how the [Feferman–Vaught theorem](/RYMZZ2AWFT/) enters.

15. • Use [Frayne's theorem](/QPX9HG9BH4/) to show that if $M \equiv N$ and $N$ is finite, then $M \cong N$.

16. • Let $U$ be a nonprincipal ultrafilter on $\N$ and $d : \Z \to \Z^{\N}/U$ the diagonal map for $(\Z, <)$. Show that the image $d(\Z)$ is convex: if $d(a) < x < d(b)$, then $x$ is in the image. Show that it is neither an initial segment nor a final segment.

17. ★ Let $^*\R = \R^{\N}/U$ for a nonprincipal ultrafilter $U$, as an ordered field. Is the set of infinitesimals definable in $^*\R$ by a first-order formula with parameters? Prove your answer.

18. ★ Let $U$ be a nonprincipal ultrafilter on $\N$ and suppose $[a_0] < [a_1] < [a_2] < \cdots < [b]$ in $(\Q, <)^{\N}/U$. Show that there is $c$ with $[a_k] < [c] < [b]$ for every $k$. Which result on [saturation of ultraproducts](/DYXQP8WS63/) is this a case of?

19. ★ Let $^*\N$ be the ultrapower of $(\N, +, \cdot, <)$ over a nonprincipal ultrafilter on $\N$. Call two nonstandard elements equivalent when their difference is a standard natural number. Show that the classes, ordered in the obvious way, form a dense linear order without endpoints.

20. ★ An ultrafilter $U$ on $I$ is countably complete if the intersection of any countably many members of $U$ is in $U$. Show that for such $U$ the diagonal map from $(\N, <)$ to $(\N, <)^I/U$ is an isomorphism.
