Take one field $\F_p$ for each prime $p$ and form sequences $(a_2, a_3, a_5, \ldots)$ with $a_p \in \F_p$. Fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter $U$ on the set of primes, and call two sequences equal when they agree at a set of primes in $U$. The classes form a field. Its characteristic is $0$: for each prime $q$, the sentence $1 + \cdots + 1 = 0$ with $q$ ones fails in every $\F_p$ with $p \neq q$, and that set of primes is cofinite, so it lies in $U$. The field is infinite for the same reason. Yet every sentence that holds in all but finitely many $\F_p$ also holds in it. Whether $-1$ is a square there depends on $U$: it is a square exactly when $U$ contains the set of primes $p$ with $p = 2$ or $p \equiv 1 \pmod 4$.

This is an [ultraproduct](/AX9Q86GB8M/), and the rule that a sentence holds in it exactly when it holds in almost all factors is [Łoś's theorem](/AGYBSTD7BG/). The chapter builds the construction, uses it to prove compactness, and then turns it into a tool for comparing structures and theories.

## What the chapter covers

The chapter is in five groups, read in order.

1. **Reduced products and ultraproducts.** A [reduced product](/F4WRD396PB/) identifies sequences that agree on a set in a filter. Over an ultrafilter it becomes an [ultraproduct](/AX9Q86GB8M/), and [Łoś's theorem](/AGYBSTD7BG/) computes its first-order theory from the factors.
2. **Compactness and ultrapowers.** [Compactness via ultraproducts](/EY09JT7270/) gives a model of a finitely satisfiable theory as an ultraproduct of models of its finite parts. An [ultrapower](/5ZVJK0V0GF/) is an ultraproduct of copies of one structure, and the diagonal map is an elementary embedding into it.
3. **Elementary equivalence and elementary classes.** The [Keisler-Shelah theorem](/A37HPCKCE2/) says that two structures are elementarily equivalent exactly when they have isomorphic ultrapowers. [Frayne's theorem](/QPX9HG9BH4/) gives an elementary embedding into an ultrapower instead. The [characterization of elementary classes](/4HNAJQQ8Z0/) follows.
4. **Products.** The [Feferman-Vaught theorem](/RYMZZ2AWFT/) reduces the theory of a product to the theories of its factors and the theory of the Boolean algebra of index sets.
5. **Saturation and Keisler's order.** [Saturation of ultraproducts](/DYXQP8WS63/) shows that countably incomplete ultraproducts realize many types. [Keisler's order](/TD5D2G5PYP/) uses this to rank complete theories.

## A first reading

A short course takes these entries in order.

- [Reduced product](/F4WRD396PB/), for the definition and the role of the filter.
- [Ultraproduct](/AX9Q86GB8M/), with its example of the field of characteristic $0$ built from the finite prime fields.
- [Łoś's theorem](/AGYBSTD7BG/) and its proof by induction on formulas. This is the centre of the chapter.
- [Compactness via ultraproducts](/EY09JT7270/), the first application.
- [Ultrapower](/5ZVJK0V0GF/), with the hyperreal numbers as an example.
- [Frayne's theorem](/QPX9HG9BH4/), which has a short proof from Łoś's theorem.
- [Characterization of elementary classes](/4HNAJQQ8Z0/), the main structural consequence.

At first the [Keisler-Shelah theorem](/A37HPCKCE2/) can be read for its statement only; its proof needs good ultrafilters or Shelah's combinatorics. The [Feferman-Vaught theorem](/RYMZZ2AWFT/) is independent of the rest and can wait. [Saturation of ultraproducts](/DYXQP8WS63/) and [Keisler's order](/TD5D2G5PYP/) need types and saturated models, and they lead to classification theory rather than back into this chapter.

## Before you start

The chapter assumes the following.

- [Filters](/15PF73BKY6/) and [ultrafilters on a set](/SWP7PEZX30/), and the [ultrafilter lemma](/3MKZST79MK/), which gives nonprincipal ultrafilters on every infinite set.
- [Completeness, compactness and interpolation](/FBWW4J1XWG/), for the statement of the [compactness theorem](/1NR3HAWG9D/).
- [Elementary extensions and the Löwenheim-Skolem theorems](/2RE7GWWXM5/), for [elementary embeddings](/CGH8C8EA9N/) and [elementary equivalence](/P3B1V182N4/).
- [Cardinal arithmetic](/SEMEEYYWT0/), for the size of an ultrapower.
- For the last group, [types](/9V71Q3SD1G/) and [saturated models](/RMTZ5J64FX/).
