1. ◦ For each complete theory, say whether it is $\aleph_0$-categorical and whether it is $\aleph_1$-categorical, in the sense of [categorical theory](/1JJCGNX240/). (a) An equivalence relation with exactly three classes, all infinite. (b) An infinite set with a unary predicate $P$ such that $P$ and its complement are both infinite. (c) An infinite set with a unary predicate $P$ such that the complement of $P$ has exactly two elements. (d) An equivalence relation with infinitely many classes, each of exactly three elements.

2. ◦ For each theory, say which hypotheses of the [Łoś–Vaught test](/ZR7Y5C235P/) hold and whether the theory is complete. (a) An equivalence relation with exactly two classes, with no other axioms. (b) The theory of torsion-free divisible abelian groups, the trivial group included. (c) The theory of algebraically closed fields, with no characteristic fixed. (d) The theory of dense linear orders without endpoints in the signature $\set{<, c}$ with one constant $c$, with no axiom about $c$.

3. ◦ Let $T$ be the [theory of dense linear orders](/2W4S2H4VYA/) without endpoints. Find $\abs{S_1(T)}$, $\abs{S_2(T)}$ and $\abs{S_3(T)}$. How many formulas in the free variables $x_1, x_2$ are there up to equivalence modulo $T$? Which clause of the [Ryll-Nardzewski theorem](/1513J40TKA/) does this illustrate?

4. ◦ Let $T$ be the theory of an equivalence relation $E$ with exactly two classes, both infinite. List the complete $2$-types of $T$, and count the complete $3$-types. Describe the orbits of the automorphism group of the countable model on triples.

5. ◦ Let $T = \operatorname{Th}(\N, S)$, where $S$ is the successor function. Which of the models $\N$, $\N + \Z$ and $\N + \Z + \Z$ (a copy of $\N$ followed by disjoint copies of $\Z$) are [atomic](/HSJ4F9J8SM/)? Which are [prime](/S8W40PYNHX/)? Name a non-isolated type that the others realize.

6. ◦ Find the error in each argument. (a) "$\operatorname{Th}(\Z, <)$ has $2^{\aleph_0}$ countable models. A theory with fewer than $2^{\aleph_0}$ countable models is small. So $\operatorname{Th}(\Z, <)$ is not small." (b) "An $\aleph_0$-categorical theory is small, and its countable model is both prime and saturated. So every small theory has a countable model that is both prime and saturated."

7. ◦ For each number $k$, say whether some complete theory with infinite models in a countable language has exactly $k$ countable models up to isomorphism. Give an example or the theorem that rules it out, or say that the question is open. (a) $1$. (b) $2$. (c) $3$. (d) $7$. (e) $\aleph_0$. (f) $\aleph_1$, assuming the continuum hypothesis. (g) $\aleph_1$, assuming $\aleph_1 < 2^{\aleph_0}$. (h) $2^{\aleph_0}$.

8. ◦ Let $T$ be [Ehrenfeucht's theory](/XT2D23G98S/): dense linear orders without endpoints with constants $c_0 < c_1 < c_2 < \cdots$. List the complete $1$-types of $T$. Which of them are isolated, and by which formula? Is $S_1(T)$ finite?

9. • Show that $(\Z, <)$ is the prime model of $\operatorname{Th}(\Z, <)$. Use the fact, from the entry on [small theories](/W6XM5NVNRP/), that the type of a tuple is fixed by the order of its entries and by the distances between them that are finite. Then describe the images of all elementary embeddings of $(\Z, <)$ into a model $\Z \cdot I$.

10. • Let $T$ be the theory of nonzero [vector spaces over $\Q$](/97YPZ0YHEB/). (a) Show that $S_1(T)$ has two elements and that $S_2(T)$ is countably infinite, and find the one $2$-type that is not isolated. (b) Show that the space of dimension $1$ is the prime model and the only atomic model. (c) Show that the space of dimension $\aleph_0$ is the countable saturated model and that no space of finite dimension is saturated.

11. • Use clause 3 of the [Ryll-Nardzewski theorem](/1513J40TKA/) to show that each theory is not $\aleph_0$-categorical, by giving infinitely many formulas in a fixed number of variables that are pairwise inequivalent modulo the theory. (a) $\mathrm{ACF}_0$, with one variable. (b) Nonzero vector spaces over $\Q$, with two variables; explain why one variable does not suffice. (c) Ehrenfeucht's theory, with one variable.

12. • In $(\Q, <)$, find all subsets of $\Q$ that are definable with the single parameter $0$, all subsets definable with the parameters $0$ and $1$, and all subsets of $\Q^2$ that are definable with no parameters. Justify the answer with the automorphism group, using that naming finitely many elements keeps $\aleph_0$-categoricity.

13. • Let $T = \operatorname{Th}(\Z, s)$, where $s$ is the successor function. Use the [Łoś–Vaught test](/ZR7Y5C235P/) with $\kappa = \aleph_1$ to prove that $T$ is complete. Then show that $T$ has exactly $\aleph_0$ countable models, and identify its prime model and its countable saturated model.

14. • Let $T$ be the theory of dense linear orders without endpoints with constants $c_i$ for $i \in \Z$ and the axioms $c_i < c_{i+1}$ for all $i \in \Z$. Show that $T$ is complete and that it has exactly $9$ countable models. Which one is prime, and which one is saturated?

15. • Let $L = \set{P_0, P_1, P_2, \ldots}$ with each $P_i$ unary, and let $T$ say that the $P_i$ are pairwise disjoint and that each $P_i$ is infinite. Show that $T$ is complete and small, find its non-isolated $1$-type, and show that $T$ has exactly $\aleph_0$ countable models. Identify the prime model and the countable saturated model. Compare with the theory of [independent unary predicates](/9MV9VG8G67/).

16. • Let $T$ be $\aleph_0$-categorical and let $M$ be its countable model. Show directly from the [Ryll-Nardzewski theorem](/1513J40TKA/) that $M$ is saturated: every type over a finite set $A \subseteq M$ is realized in $M$.

17. • Let $T = \operatorname{Th}(\N, 0, 1, +, \cdot, <)$. For each set $X$ of primes, show that the set of formulas "$p$ divides $x$" for $p \in X$ and "$p$ does not divide $x$" for primes $p \notin X$ is consistent with $T$. Conclude that $T$ is not small and that it has $2^{\aleph_0}$ countable models. Show that $\N$ is nevertheless a prime model of $T$.

18. • Show that the theory of dense linear orders without endpoints is finitely axiomatizable and that the theory of an [equivalence relation with infinitely many infinite classes](/N232QBKCAA/) is not, although both are $\aleph_0$-categorical.

19. • Let $T$ be a complete theory with infinite models in a countable language. (a) Show that if every countable model of $T$ is atomic, then $T$ is $\aleph_0$-categorical. (b) Show that $T$ has a countable model that is both prime and saturated exactly when $T$ is $\aleph_0$-categorical.

20. ★ For a countable nonempty linear order $I$, show that the model $\Z \cdot I$ of $\operatorname{Th}(\Z, <)$ is saturated if and only if $I$ is dense without endpoints, that is, if and only if $I \cong \Q$.

21. ★ Let $T$ be complete in a countable language, and let $M$ and $N$ be countable atomic models of $T$. Prove that $M \cong N$ by a [back-and-forth](/6F2DB4HTJR/) construction. Where does the proof use that the types are isolated, and where that $T$ is complete?

22. ★ Let $T$ be a small complete theory in a countable language that is not $\aleph_0$-categorical. Prove that $T$ has a countable model that is neither prime nor saturated, so that $T$ has at least three countable models. This is the core of [Vaught's never-two theorem](/E5W3RRSY5Z/).

23. ★ Add to Ehrenfeucht's theory two unary predicates $R$ and $B$ with axioms saying that every element is in exactly one of them, that between any two elements there are elements of both, and that every $c_i$ is in $R$. Show that the theory is complete and has exactly $4$ countable models.

24. ★ Let $T$ be the theory of dense linear orders without endpoints with a constant $c_q$ for each $q \in \Q$ and the axioms $c_q < c_r$ for $q < r$. Show that $T$ is complete, that $\abs{S_1(T)} = 2^{\aleph_0}$, and that $T$ has $2^{\aleph_0}$ countable models. Show that $T$ has a prime model but no countable saturated model. Compare the count with Ehrenfeucht's theory and with the theory of constants indexed by $\Z$ above.
