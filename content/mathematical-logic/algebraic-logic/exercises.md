1. ◦ Count the elements of the [Lindenbaum–Tarski algebra](/QC9M3Q8B8Q/) of classical logic in the three variables $p, q, r$ for (a) the empty theory, (b) the theory $\set{p \to q}$, (c) the theory $\set{p \land q}$.

2. ◦ Let $W = \set{a, b, c, d}$ be preordered by $a \le b \le d$ and $a \le c \le d$, and let $I X = \set{w : v \in X \text{ for all } v \ge w}$ be the interior operator of its up-set topology. Compute $I\set{a, b, d}$, list the open elements, and say whether the open elements form a chain.

3. ◦ Let $W = \set{0, 1}$ and $R = \set{(0, 1), (1, 0)}$. In the complex algebra of the frame $(W, R)$, compute $\Box X$ for each $X \subseteq W$. Which of $\Box x \le x$ and $\Box \Box x = x$ hold in this [modal algebra](/3H85NSFWHK/)?

4. ◦ In the five-element [MV-algebra](/8T4M7RN9AC/) $\set{0, \frac14, \frac12, \frac34, 1}$ with $x \oplus y = \min(1, x + y)$ and $\lnot x = 1 - x$, find the elements with $x \oplus x = x$ and the elements with $\lnot x = x$. What is the least $n$ with $\frac34 \odot \cdots \odot \frac34 = 0$ ($n$ factors)?

5. ◦ The product t-norm on $[0,1]$ is $x \otimes y = xy$. Find its residuum $x \to y$ and compute $\frac12 \to \frac15$. Is $[0,1]$ with this operation an MV-algebra? A Gödel algebra?

6. ◦ On $U = \set{1, 2, 3}$, let $R = \set{(1, 2), (2, 3), (3, 1)}$ in the full [relation algebra](/GQK4DSA11K/). Compute $R ; R$, $\breve{R}$ and $R ; R ; R$, and check whether $\breve{R} ; R \le 1'$.

7. ◦ Let $U = \set{0, 1, 2}$ and consider the full two-dimensional [cylindric](/1MDT9BF81H/) set algebra on $U \times U$. How many elements does it have? Compute $c_0 \set{(0, 1)}$ and $c_1 \set{(0, 1)}$. How many elements $X$ satisfy $c_0 X = X$, and how many satisfy both $c_0 X = X$ and $c_1 X = X$?

8. ◦ In the three-element Heyting algebra $0 < h < 1$, use part 4 of the [bridge theorems](/C3DC1C23SQ/) to find the classes of the congruence generated by the pair $(h, 1)$.

9. • How many operators $\Box$ on the four-element Boolean algebra satisfy $\Box 1 = 1$ and $\Box(x \wedge y) = \Box x \wedge \Box y$? How many of them also satisfy $\Box x \le x$ and $\Box \Box x = \Box x$, so that they make it an [interior algebra](/BDEX7HET71/)?

10. • Let $W = \set{0, 1, 2}$ and $R = \set{(0, 1), (1, 2)}$. In the complex algebra of $(W, R)$, find every $X \subseteq W$ with $\Box X \not\le \Box \Box X$. Explain the answer by the failure of transitivity.

11. • List all subalgebras of the MV-algebra $\Gamma(\Z, 6)$ described in [Mundici's equivalence](/64NDFWQEZ2/).

12. • Let $G = \Z^2$ with the coordinatewise order and $u = (2, 3)$. Describe the MV-algebra $\Gamma(G, u)$: how many elements does it have, which elements satisfy $x \oplus x = x$, and is it a Boolean algebra?

13. • On the chain $\set{0, \frac13, \frac23, 1}$, let $x \otimes y = \min(x, y)$ if $x + y > 1$ and $x \otimes y = 0$ otherwise (the nilpotent minimum). Compute the residuum, show that the structure is prelinear, and find every pair $(x, y)$ at which divisibility $x \otimes (x \to y) = x \wedge y$ fails. Is it a [BL-algebra](/ERNYMVEGKC/)?

14. • In a [residuated lattice](/E337A6WVH3/), prove that $x(y \vee z) = xy \vee xz$ and $x \backslash (y \wedge z) = (x \backslash y) \wedge (x \backslash z)$.

15. • Let $X$ be a four-element set. How many elements does the [Post algebra](/RD2A5Z7E2B/) $P_3^X$ have, and how many of them have a lattice complement? Check the first count against the representation $x = (b_1 \wedge e_1) \vee (b_2 \wedge e_2)$ with Boolean elements $b_1 \ge b_2$.

16. • In the [Łukasiewicz–Moisil algebra](/51A2GMPDYK/) $L_4 = \set{0, \frac13, \frac23, 1}$, tabulate $\varphi_1, \varphi_2, \varphi_3$ and $\sim$. Show that $x$ is determined by the triple $(\varphi_1 x, \varphi_2 x, \varphi_3 x)$, and describe the triples that occur.

17. • Let $B = \set{0, a, b, 1}$ be the four-element Boolean algebra. How many elements does the twist structure $B^{\bowtie}$ of [Nelson algebras](/XRGDT262HQ/) have? Which elements satisfy $\sim x = x$, and which satisfy $x \le \sim x$?

18. • Following [relation algebras and three-variable logic](/AHWXJ3GT02/), write each property of a relation $R$ as an equation or inequality in the full relation algebra: (a) symmetric, (b) reflexive, (c) a partial function, (d) antisymmetric, (e) every point has an $R$-successor.

19. • Compute the [Leibniz congruence](/6JT3CMJT8F/) of (a) the filter $F = \set{X : \set{1, 2} \subseteq X}$ of the Boolean algebra of subsets of $\set{1, 2, 3}$, (b) the designated set $\set{\mathbf{T}, \mathbf{B}}$ of the four-element De Morgan algebra of [first-degree entailment](/X4HG2793GD/).

20. ★ In an interior algebra, start from an element $x$ and apply $I$ and $\lnot$ in any order, any number of times. Show that at most $14$ distinct elements arise.

21. ★ Find a relation algebra term $t$ without variables such that, in the full relation algebra on a nonempty set $U$, $t = 1$ if $U$ has at least three elements and $t = 0$ otherwise.

22. ★ Let LP be the logic of the three-element Kleene chain $0 < \frac12 < 1$ with $\land = \min$, $\lor = \max$, $\lnot x = 1 - x$ and designated set $\set{\frac12, 1}$. Unlike the conjunction and disjunction fragment, LP has theorems, such as $p \lor \lnot p$. Show that LP is nevertheless not protoalgebraic.

23. ★ In the Post algebra $P_3 = \set{0, 1, 2}$ with $x' = x + 1$ for $x < 2$ and $2' = 0$, show that every function $P_3 \to P_3$ is given by a term in $\wedge$, $\vee$, $'$ and the constant $0$.

24. ★ Let $W$ be a finite set. Show that the operators $\Box$ on the power set of $W$ with $\Box W = W$ and $\Box(X \cap Y) = \Box X \cap \Box Y$ are exactly the operators $\Box_R X = \set{w : R(w) \subseteq X}$ for relations $R$ on $W$, and that different relations give different operators. How many such operators are there when $\abs{W} = 3$?
