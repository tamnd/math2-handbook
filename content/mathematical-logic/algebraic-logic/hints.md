3. $\Box \emptyset = \emptyset$, $\Box\set{0} = \set{1}$, $\Box\set{1} = \set{0}$ and $\Box W = W$. So $\Box x \le x$ fails at $x = \set{0}$, while $\Box \Box x = x$ holds for every $x$, because $R$ is a bijection of order two.

4. $x \oplus x = x$ only for $0$ and $1$, and $\lnot x = x$ only for $\frac12$. The powers of $\frac34$ are $\frac34, \frac12, \frac14, 0$, so $n = 4$.

7. It has $2^9 = 512$ elements. $c_0\set{(0, 1)} = \set{(0, 1), (1, 1), (2, 1)}$ and $c_1\set{(0, 1)} = \set{(0, 0), (0, 1), (0, 2)}$. The sets with $c_0 X = X$ are $U \times Y$ for $Y \subseteq U$, so there are $8$; only $\emptyset$ and $U \times U$ are fixed by both.

9. By the finite case of the [Jónsson–Tarski theorem](/HC5V2QFFWB/), each such operator is $\Box_R$ for exactly one relation $R$ on the two atoms, so there are $2^4 = 16$. The interior operators correspond to the reflexive and transitive relations, that is, the $4$ preorders on a two-element set.

11. A subalgebra contains $0$ and $6$ and is closed under $\oplus$ and $6 - x$, so it is generated by its least nonzero element $k$, and closure under $6 - x$ forces $k \mid 6$. The subalgebras are $\set{0, 6}$, $\set{0, 3, 6}$, $\set{0, 2, 4, 6}$ and the whole chain.

13. Compute $x \to y = \max\set{z : x \otimes z \le y}$. Every chain is prelinear. Divisibility fails only at $x = \frac23$, $y = \frac13$: there $x \to y = \frac13$ and $\frac23 \otimes \frac13 = 0 \ne \frac13$. So it is not a BL-algebra.

17. Each atom of $B$ lies in the first coordinate, in the second, or in neither, so $B^{\bowtie}$ has $3^2 = 9$ elements. Only $(0, 0)$ is fixed by $\sim$, and $x \le \sim x$ holds exactly for the four elements $(0, c)$.

19. (a) The congruence of the filter, $X \mathrel{\theta} Y$ iff $X \cap \set{1, 2} = Y \cap \set{1, 2}$, with four classes of two elements; it is the largest congruence compatible with $F$. (b) The identity. The formula $x$ separates designated from undesignated values, and $\lnot x$ separates $\mathbf{F}$ from $\mathbf{N}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ from $\mathbf{T}$, so the matrix is reduced.

20. Put $C x = \lnot I \lnot x$. From $I x \le x$, idempotence and monotonicity, show $I C I C x = I C x$. Then the words in $I$ and $C$ applied to $x$ give at most $7$ elements, namely $x$, $Ix$, $Cx$, $ICx$, $CIx$, $ICIx$ and $CICx$, and their complements give at most $7$ more.

21. Take $t = 1 ; (0' \wedge (0' ; 0')) ; 1$. The middle term contains $(a, b)$ iff $a \ne b$ and some $c$ differs from both, which needs three points; composing with $1$ on both sides turns any nonempty relation into $1$.

22. Order the values by information, with $0$ and $1$ below $\frac12$; the operations $\min$, $\max$ and $1 - x$ are monotone for this order. If $\vdash \delta(x, x, \vec{z})$, compare the valuation $x = y = 0$ with the valuation $x = \frac12$, $y = 0$, and conclude that $\Delta(x, y, \vec{z})$ and $x$ are designated while $y$ is not.

23. Check that $(x \vee x')'$, $(x \vee x'')'$ and $(x' \vee x'')'$ take the value $2$ at $0$, $1$ and $2$ respectively and $0$ elsewhere. Then $f(x)$ is the join over $j$ of $c_{f(j)}$ meet the $j$-th of these terms, with the constants $c_0 = 0$, $c_1 = 0'$ and $c_2 = 0''$.
