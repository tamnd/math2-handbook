Ordinal analysis measures the strength of a formal theory by an ordinal. The model is [Gentzen's consistency proof](/QPV0HNP22S/) of 1936. Gentzen fixed a primitive recursive notation system for the ordinals below [$\varepsilon_0$](/KT50SMH3EC/), the least ordinal $\varepsilon$ with $\omega^\varepsilon = \varepsilon$. Its terms are nested Cantor normal forms such as $\omega^{\omega^\omega + 1} + \omega^3 \cdot 2 + 5$, and two terms are compared by comparing their exponents first. He showed that [primitive recursive arithmetic](/WCXH72T8D8/), together with [transfinite induction](/0ZHQMQ2C1N/) along this notation system for quantifier-free formulas, proves the consistency of [Peano arithmetic](/0QFT7Z76N5/). Every other step of the proof is finitary. Since $\mathrm{PA}$ does not prove its own consistency, it cannot prove this induction. In 1943 Gentzen also showed that it proves transfinite induction up to each ordinal below $\varepsilon_0$. So $\varepsilon_0$ is exactly the length of induction that $\mathrm{PA}$ cannot reach, its [proof-theoretic ordinal](/C72RBH0CDR/).

The same programme applies to stronger theories, and each gets a larger ordinal: $\omega^\omega$ for $\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$, $\Gamma_0$ for $\mathrm{ATR}_0$, the Bachmann–Howard ordinal for the theory $\mathrm{ID}_1$ of one inductive definition. The ordinals soon outgrow Cantor normal form, and naming them needs new notation systems. The [Veblen functions](/JQFAE9S2F4/) enumerate fixed points and name the ordinals up to $\Gamma_0$. The ordinal collapsing functions, such as the [Buchholz psi functions](/E7WEFRMQTB/), use names of uncountable ordinals to name large countable ones. The analysis itself often works with infinitary derivations: the induction axioms are replaced by the $\omega$-rule, the cuts are eliminated, and the height of the result gives the bound. An ordinal analysis yields more than a consistency proof. It also gives the functions that the theory proves total, independence results such as [Goodstein's theorem](/XQMNJF241J/), and a way to compare theories by a single ordinal.

## What the chapter covers

The chapter is in six groups, read in order.

1. **The aim and the scale.** [Ordinal analysis](/C5E5TG8PGP/) states the programme and its three parts: a notation system, a lower bound and an upper bound. An [ordinal notation system](/7MBX59Y7N9/) is a computable well-order. Its examples show why Kleene's $\mathcal{O}$ is not one, and why a notation system must be natural and not just computable.
2. **Epsilon nought and Peano arithmetic.** [Epsilon nought](/KT50SMH3EC/) and its fundamental sequences, [Gentzen's consistency proof](/QPV0HNP22S/), [transfinite induction in arithmetic](/ZE54ABPMJ0/) with Gentzen's jump, and the definition of the [proof-theoretic ordinal](/C72RBH0CDR/), with $\abs{\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1} = \omega^\omega$ and $\abs{\mathrm{ACA}} = \varepsilon_{\varepsilon_0}$ as examples.
3. **Infinitary derivations.** The [ω-rule](/XAMKAB1BZM/) and the infinitary system for arithmetic, with embedding, cut elimination and the bound $\varepsilon_0$.
4. **Predicative notations.** The [Veblen function](/JQFAE9S2F4/), the [Feferman–Schütte ordinal](/CNN7BK5YAW/) $\Gamma_0$, and the [small and large Veblen ordinals](/ZHDM657G3D/).
5. **Collapsing functions.** The [Bachmann–Howard ordinal](/QH3ENXFRSV/), the [ordinal collapsing function](/8S6CT4TMB7/) $\psi$, the [Buchholz psi functions](/E7WEFRMQTB/), [Rathjen's psi function](/M7D1HEJQVH/) for a Mahlo cardinal, and the [Takeuti–Feferman–Buchholz ordinal](/NZWRK062WQ/).
6. **Impredicative theories.** [Theories of iterated inductive definitions](/5VNT564Y2W/), the [ordinal analysis of Π¹₁ comprehension](/2MGJJTSRFD/), [Takeuti's conjecture](/W560YMCPA3/) on cut elimination in second-order logic, and [local predicativity and Buchholz's Ω-rule](/5C4YBXQV15/).

## A first reading

For a first reading, take these entries in order: [ordinal analysis](/C5E5TG8PGP/), [ordinal notation system](/7MBX59Y7N9/), [epsilon nought](/KT50SMH3EC/), [Gentzen's consistency proof](/QPV0HNP22S/), [transfinite induction in arithmetic](/ZE54ABPMJ0/), [proof-theoretic ordinal](/C72RBH0CDR/), [ω-rule and infinitary derivations](/XAMKAB1BZM/), [Veblen function](/JQFAE9S2F4/), [Feferman–Schütte ordinal](/CNN7BK5YAW/) and [ordinal collapsing function](/8S6CT4TMB7/). This covers Peano arithmetic completely and gives the ideas behind every later notation. The attached examples on small values of the collapsing function, and the example where the function without $\Omega$ stops at $\zeta_0$, show the trick of collapsing more clearly than any definition.

The large Veblen ordinals, Rathjen's function and the Takeuti–Feferman–Buchholz ordinal can be skipped. The last group is research level. Read the [theories of iterated inductive definitions](/5VNT564Y2W/) first, and the Ω-rule after it; Takeuti's conjecture can be read on its own.

## Before you start

You need [ordinal numbers](/0YSCSX5YZC/), [well-orders](/01CGSJ0GNQ/) and [Cantor normal form](/6GD30FGYDZ/), and from [ordinal arithmetic and normal functions](/Z092EDNW9B/) the [normal functions](/ERTAM0SFVB/) and their fixed points. On the side of proof theory you need the [sequent calculus](/W72CA3R9MP/) and the [cut-elimination theorem](/AYRZ98Y0NW/) from [structural proof theory](/R10RMQCJQX/), and [Peano arithmetic](/0QFT7Z76N5/) with its [fragments](/T70PYM1F36/). [Gödel's second incompleteness theorem](/4PZM5GJTR5/) explains why a consistency proof must use something the theory lacks. The groups on $\mathrm{ATR}_0$ and $\Pi^1_1$-comprehension assume the subsystems of [second-order arithmetic](/DC60FYCSPM/) from [reverse mathematics](/7SQS69T17R/).
