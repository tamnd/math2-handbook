## History

In a short paper of 1933, Gödel read intuitionistic logic in a modal logic of provability, essentially S4, and remarked that for provability in a formal system the law $\square p \to p$ cannot be proved inside the system, by his second incompleteness theorem. Henkin asked in 1952 whether a sentence that asserts its own provability is provable, and Löb answered him in "Solution of a problem of Leon Henkin" (1955). His proof isolated the [derivability conditions](/F6H4STF9RG/), which are the modal laws of [provability logic](/WJK0YA4GP2/).

The modal logic $\mathsf{GL}$ was studied in the early 1970s, and Segerberg proved in 1971 that it is complete for finite transitive irreflexive frames. Solovay proved the [arithmetical completeness](/1ZRWVDYPX0/) of $\mathsf{GL}$ and $\mathsf{GLS}$ in "Provability interpretations of modal logic" (Israel Journal of Mathematics, 1976). In the mid-1970s, de Jongh and Sambin independently found explicit fixed points, the [fixed-point theorem](/TDDX1NHG8D/), and Bernardi, de Jongh and Sambin independently proved their uniqueness. Magari and the Siena school introduced diagonalizable algebras, now called [Magari algebras](/PHWAN904PT/), in the same years.

Grzegorczyk introduced his logic in 1967. Its reading as the logic of "true and provable" was found independently by Goldblatt, by Boolos and by Kuznetsov and Muravitsky. Japaridze defined the [polymodal logic GLP](/3EDRQJJHMT/) and proved its arithmetical completeness in his 1986 dissertation. Ignatiev simplified the logic and described its closed fragment, and from 2004 Beklemishev used GLP to give an ordinal analysis of PA, with worms as ordinal notations below $\varepsilon_0$.

Visser introduced [interpretability logic](/BGKZTSQN3W/) in the late 1980s, and de Jongh and Veltman gave the semantics now named after Veltman. Shavrukov (1988) and Berarducci (1990) independently proved that $\mathsf{ILM}$ is the interpretability logic of PA, and Visser proved that $\mathsf{ILP}$ is the interpretability logic of finitely axiomatized theories such as GB. For [quantified provability logic](/5K9J8R8SCP/), Artemov showed in 1985 that the logic of truth is not arithmetical, and Vardanyan showed in 1986 that the provability logic of PA is $\Pi^0_2$-complete.

## Further reading

- George Boolos, *The Logic of Provability* (Cambridge University Press, 1993). The standard textbook, with full proofs of the Kripke completeness, Solovay's theorems and the fixed-point theorem.
- C. Smoryński, *Self-Reference and Modal Logic* (Springer, 1985). Treats provability logic together with the arithmetic behind it, including fixed points and $\mathsf{GLS}$.
- Giorgi Japaridze and Dick de Jongh, "The logic of provability", in Samuel R. Buss (ed.), *Handbook of Proof Theory* (Elsevier, 1998). A survey of provability and interpretability logics, including GLP.
- Sergei Artemov and Lev Beklemishev, "Provability logic", in D. M. Gabbay and F. Guenthner (eds.), *Handbook of Philosophical Logic*, 2nd edition, volume 13 (Springer, 2005). A survey that covers the later work on GLP and its use in proof theory.
- Albert Visser, "An overview of interpretability logic", in *Advances in Modal Logic*, volume 1 (CSLI Publications, 1998). The main survey of interpretability logics and their arithmetical completeness.
