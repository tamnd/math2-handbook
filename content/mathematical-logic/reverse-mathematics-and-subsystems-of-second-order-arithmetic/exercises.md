1. ◦ For each theorem, name the system of the big five to which it is equivalent over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$, or say that $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ proves it: (a) the [Heine–Borel theorem](/07PFYV65MB/) for countable covers of $[0, 1]$; (b) the [Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem](/0KDJD249DT/); (c) any two countable well-orderings are comparable; (d) every closed set of reals is the union of a perfect set and a countable set; (e) the intermediate value theorem; (f) every countable vector space over $\Q$ has a basis; (g) every countable commutative ring has a maximal ideal; (h) every countable commutative ring has a prime ideal.

2. ◦ True or false: (a) the computable sets form an $\omega$-model of [$\mathsf{WKL}_0$](/7W9EG02T79/); (b) the arithmetical sets form an $\omega$-model of [$\mathsf{ATR}_0$](/MKB74TX3Z8/); (c) the hyperarithmetical sets form an $\omega$-model of $\mathsf{ATR}_0$; (d) the full power set of $\N$ forms an $\omega$-model of [$\Pi^1_1\text{-}\mathsf{CA}_0$](/T21WETAHN5/).

3. ◦ Which of the following collections are [Turing ideals](/Y6D27XJT3H/)? (a) the sets computable from a fixed set $A$; (b) the low sets; (c) the hyperarithmetical sets; (d) the computably enumerable sets; (e) the finite sets.

4. ◦ Which of the following does [$\mathsf{RCA}_0$](/XQENB067R3/) prove? (a) the induction scheme for $\Sigma^0_1$ formulas; (b) the induction scheme for $\Pi^0_1$ formulas; (c) the intermediate value theorem; (d) the induction scheme for $\Sigma^0_2$ formulas; (e) every set has a Turing jump.

5. ◦ True or false, with all statements over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$: (a) $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ proves $\mathrm{RT}^2_2$; (b) $\mathrm{RT}^2_2$ implies $\mathsf{ACA}_0$; (c) $\mathrm{RT}^2_2$ implies weak König's lemma; (d) $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ proves $\mathrm{RT}^2_2$; (e) $\mathrm{RT}^2_2$ implies Ramsey's theorem for pairs and three colors.

6. ◦ True or false: (a) every [$\beta$-model](/J0Z2GNJZ2W/) is an $\omega$-model; (b) the arithmetical sets form a $\beta$-model; (c) the full power set of $\N$ forms a $\beta$-model.

7. ◦ Give the proof-theoretic ordinal and the first-order part (as a fragment of PA where one applies) of $\mathsf{RCA}_0$, $\mathsf{WKL}_0$, [$\mathsf{ACA}_0$](/NAASTN80QN/), $\mathsf{ATR}_0$ and $\Pi^1_1\text{-}\mathsf{CA}_0$.

8. • Show in $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ that every infinite set $X \subseteq \N$ has an increasing enumeration, that is, a strictly increasing function $p$ whose range is $X$.

9. • Build a computable infinite binary tree with no computable path, using the two disjoint c.e. sets $\set{e : \varphi_e(e) = 0}$ and $\set{e : \varphi_e(e) = 1}$.

10. • Show over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ that the monotone convergence theorem for bounded increasing sequences of rationals implies $\mathsf{ACA}_0$, using the sequence $a_n = \sum_{i \le n} 2^{-f(i)}$ for an injection $f$.

11. • Show that for each standard $k \ge 2$, $\mathsf{RCA}_0 + \mathrm{RT}^2_2$ proves $\mathrm{RT}^2_k$, Ramsey's theorem for pairs and $k$ colors.

12. • Build a computable binary tree of measure at least $3/4$ with no computable path, by removing for each $e$ the strings that extend $\varphi_e \restriction (e + 3)$ once this is defined. Check the measure bound.

13. • Let $E$ be Kleene's functional, with $E(f) = 0$ if $f(n) = 0$ for some $n$ and $E(f) = 1$ otherwise. Show that $E$ is not continuous at the constant function $1$, in the product topology on $\N^\N$.

14. • Show that $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ does not prove the consistency of primitive recursive arithmetic.

15. • Show in $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ that comprehension for $\Sigma^0_1$ formulas implies comprehension for all arithmetical formulas.

16. • Show that every $\omega$-model of $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ contains a complete consistent extension of Peano arithmetic.

17. ★ Show over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ that weak König's lemma is equivalent to $\Sigma^0_1$ separation: for any two $\Sigma^0_1$ formulas $\varphi_0(n)$ and $\varphi_1(n)$ that no $n$ satisfies together, there is a set $X$ with $\varphi_0(n) \to n \in X$ and $\varphi_1(n) \to n \notin X$.

18. ★ Show that the intersection of all $\omega$-models of $\mathsf{WKL}_0$ is exactly the class of computable sets.

19. ★ A coloring $c : [\N]^2 \to 2$ is stable if $\lim_y c(x, y)$ exists for every $x$. Show over $\mathsf{RCA}_0$ that stable Ramsey's theorem $\mathrm{SRT}^2_2$ together with the cohesiveness principle $\mathrm{COH}$ implies $\mathrm{RT}^2_2$.

20. ★ For a model $M$ of Peano arithmetic, let $\mathrm{Def}(M)$ be the family of subsets of $M$ definable in $M$ with parameters. Show that $(M, \mathrm{Def}(M))$ is a model of $\mathsf{ACA}_0$, and conclude that $\mathsf{ACA}_0$ is conservative over PA for arithmetic sentences.
