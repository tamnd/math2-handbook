1. ◦ Write the first four terms $G_1, \dots, G_4$ of the [Goodstein sequence](/XQMNJF241J/) of $5$, each in hereditary base $k + 1$. Replace the base by $\omega$ to get ordinals $\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_4$, and check that they strictly decrease.

2. ◦ Compute the Goodstein lengths $L(1)$ and $L(2)$, where $L(m)$ is the least $k$ with $G_k = 0$. Then check the formula $L(m) = H_{o_2(m)}(3) - 2$ for $m = 1, 2, 3$, using the values of the [Hardy hierarchy](/X8RKXQASR9/) and $L(3) = 6$.

3. ◦ A hydra for the [Kirby-Paris game](/C028FF4TWM/) has a root with two children: a head, and a node $p$ that carries two heads. Give its ordinal. Hercules cuts one head of $p$ with $N = 3$. Draw the new hydra, give its ordinal, and count the nodes before and after.

4. ◦ For [Beklemishev's worm principle](/MHDZGDNEPS/), let $L(w)$ be the least $m$ with $w_m$ empty. Compute $L(01)$, $L(100)$ and $L(101)$. Then write the worms $w_1, w_2, w_3, w_4$ of the worm sequence of $011$.

5. ◦ (a) Which of these sets are large in the sense of the [strengthened finite Ramsey theorem](/4P2BAT6YC3/): $\set{3, 5, 9}$, $\set{4, 5, 6}$, $\set{1}$, $\set{5, 6, 7, 8, 9}$? (b) Color the points of $\set{1, \dots, 8}$ red on $\set{1, 2}$, blue on $\set{3, 4}$ and green on $\set{5, \dots, 8}$. Show that no homogeneous set with at least $3$ elements is large, although one color class has $4$ elements.

6. ◦ For the [Kanamori-McAloon theorem](/NM5WYBK6BG/) with $n = 2$: (a) is $f(\set{x, y}) = y - x$ regressive on the pairs from $\set{3, 4, 5}$? On the pairs from $\set{3, \dots, 7}$? (b) Show that $f(s) = \min s - 1$, on the pairs of positive integers, is regressive and that every set is min-homogeneous for it. (c) Is any $3$-element set homogeneous for this $f$?

7. ◦ Compute $H_{\omega^2 + \omega + 2}(3)$ and $g_{\omega^2 + \omega + 2}(3)$, using the [Hardy](/X8RKXQASR9/) and [slow-growing](/VDF6R9EVJ2/) hierarchies. Then compute $f_2(3)$ and $f_3(1)$ in the [fast-growing hierarchy](/R6ZC4BJGXV/).

8. ◦ Let $M$ be a non-standard model of $\mathsf{PA}$ with standard part $\N$, and let $a \in M$ be non-standard. Which of these sets are cuts in the sense of [indicators](/JWTZ8NVVGK/), and which of the cuts are closed under $x \mapsto 2x$? (a) $\N$. (b) $\set{x : x \le a}$. (c) $\set{x : x < a + k \text{ for some } k \in \N}$. (d) $\set{x : x < k a \text{ for some } k \in \N}$. (e) $\set{x : x < a^k \text{ for some } k \in \N}$. Also compute the doubling indicator $\max\set{c : a \cdot 2^c \le b}$ for $a = 5$, $b = 1000$.

9. ◦ Which of these are [concrete incompleteness](/E2H655A7BS/) results for $T = \mathsf{PA}$? (a) Every Goodstein sequence that starts below $10^6$ reaches $0$. (b) Every Goodstein sequence reaches $0$. (c) $\mathrm{Con}(\mathsf{PA})$. (d) Kruskal's tree theorem for infinite sequences of trees. (e) The worm principle. Give the reason in each case.

10. • Run the battle on a path of $3$ edges that always cuts the rightmost head, as the standard battle does, but takes $N_n = 2$ at every step. Show that it ends after $41$ steps. Give the ordinal after steps $1$, $2$, $3$, $15$, $28$ and $37$.

11. • Prove, without the general theorem, that every Kirby-Paris battle on a hydra of height at most $2$ ends, for any choice of the numbers $N_n$. Use the ordinal $\omega^{j_1} + \cdots + \omega^{j_r}$, where $j_1 \ge \dots \ge j_r$ are the numbers of heads of the children of the root, with a head on the root counted as $\omega^0$.

12. • The weak Goodstein sequence writes $G_k$ in base $k + 1$ with the exponents left as ordinary numbers, replaces the base by $k + 2$ and subtracts $1$. Show that it always reaches $0$, with an ordinal below $\omega^\omega$, and show that from $4$ it first reaches $0$ at term $22$.

13. • Show that $L(4) = 3 \cdot 2^{402653211} - 2$, from $L(m) = H_{o_2(m)}(3) - 2$, the law $H_{\omega^\alpha} = f_\alpha$, and the value $f_2(x) = 2^x x$.

14. • Find a closed form for $H_{\omega^2 \cdot 2}(x)$ in terms of $x$, and compute $H_{\omega^2 \cdot 2}(1)$ and $H_{\omega^2 \cdot 2}(2)$.

15. • Show that each $f_n$ with $n < \omega$ is [primitive recursive](/5NVA2JRHPV/), that $f_\omega$ eventually dominates every $f_n$, and conclude that $f_\omega$ is not primitive recursive. You may use that every primitive recursive function is eventually dominated by some $f_n$.

16. • In the variant of the Kanamori-McAloon theorem with domain $\set{0, \dots, m - 1}$, a function $f$ on pairs is regressive if $f(s) < \min s$ whenever $0 \notin s$. For $n = 2$ and $k = 4$, show that the least $m$ is $6$: every such $f$ on the pairs from $\set{0, \dots, 5}$ has a min-homogeneous set of $4$ elements, and some such $f$ on the pairs from $\set{0, \dots, 4}$ has none.

17. • For the [finite graph minor theorem](/WXNTPQGJ38/) with $n = 2$, so that $G_i$ has size at most $2 + i$, find the least $m$. Give a bad sequence of length $m - 1$.

18. • Run the standard Kirby-Paris battle on the hydra whose root has one child, and that child carries two heads. How many steps does it take? Track the ordinal.

19. • Run the first three steps of the standard battle of the [Buchholz hydra](/9APRFM0G7F/) $+(0(1(1)))$, a root, a child labeled $0$, a grandchild labeled $1$ and a head labeled $1$, always cutting the rightmost head. Write each hydra as a bracket expression.

20. • For [Friedman's finite form of Kruskal's theorem](/12XRGBWH59/) with $n = 3$, take $T_1$ the path with $4$ vertices, $T_2$ the star with $4$ leaves, $T_3$ a root with two leaves and a child with two leaves, $T_4$ a root with three children each carrying one leaf, and $T_5$ a root with two children carrying two and three leaves. Check the sizes, and show that the sequence is bad. What does this give for the least $m$ in $P(3)$?

21. ★ Show that for $n = 1$ the least $N$ in the strengthened finite Ramsey theorem is $(m - 1) \cdot 2^{k - 1} + 1$, for all $k, m \ge 1$. Conclude that $\mathsf{PA}$ proves the case $n = 1$.

22. ★ Let $\mathrm{tree}(n)$ be the greatest length of a sequence of rooted trees $T_1, T_2, \dots$, with $T_i$ of at most $i + n$ vertices, in which no $T_i$ embeds into a later $T_j$. Show that $\mathrm{tree}(2) = 5$: find a bad sequence of length $5$ that starts with the root with two children, and show that no bad sequence has length $6$.

23. ★ Show that every worm sequence that starts from a worm with letters in $\set{0, 1}$ reaches the empty worm. Split $w$ at its zeros into blocks of ones of lengths $r_0, \dots, r_t$ from left to right, and use the ordinal $\omega^{r_0} + \cdots + \omega^{r_t}$, which need not be in Cantor normal form.

24. ★ Let $Y(a, b) = \max\set{c : a^{2^c} \le b}$ for $2 \le a \le b$ in a non-standard model $M$ of $\mathsf{PA}$. Show that $Y$ satisfies conditions 2 and 3 of an indicator for the class of cuts closed under $x \mapsto x^2$. Show that it is not an indicator for the class of cuts that are models of $\mathsf{PA}$.

25. ★ Show that each $f_n$ with $n < \omega$ is [provably total](/Z742YPHPNS/) in $\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$, but that $\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$ does not prove $\forall n\, \forall x\, \exists y\; f_n(x) = y$. Show that $\mathsf{PA}$ proves it.
