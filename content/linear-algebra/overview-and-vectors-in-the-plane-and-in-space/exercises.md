1. ◦ Find the [length](/0XKBKGTCPC/) of $v = (3, -4, 12)$ and the unit vector in the direction of $v$.

2. ◦ Let $u = (1, -2, 2)$ and $v = (3, 0, -4)$. Compute $u \cdot v$ and $\cos\theta$ for the [angle](/0ZEHZY85E6/) $\theta$ between them. Is $\theta$ acute, right or obtuse?

3. ◦ For which real $t$ are $(t, 2, -1)$ and $(3, t, 4)$ perpendicular?

4. ◦ Compute $(2, 0, 1) \times (1, 3, -1)$, and check with two dot products that the result is perpendicular to both factors.

5. ◦ A student writes $a \times (b \times c) = (a \times b) \times c$ for all $a, b, c \in \R^3$. Give three vectors for which the two sides differ, and compute both sides.

6. ◦ The line $L$ is $(2, -1, 0) + t(1, 3, 2)$. Decide whether $(4, 5, 4)$ and $(5, 8, 5)$ lie on $L$.

7. ◦ Write the equation of the plane through $(1, 1, 1)$ with normal vector $(2, -1, 3)$ in the form $ax + by + cz = d$, and find its distance from the origin.

8. • Write $u = (4, 1, -2)$ as $p + q$ with $p$ a multiple of $v = (2, 2, 1)$ and $q \perp v$. Check that $\norm{u}^2 = \norm{p}^2 + \norm{q}^2$.

9. • Find the area of the triangle with vertices $(1, 1, 0)$, $(3, 2, 1)$ and $(2, 4, 3)$.

10. • Find the volume of the parallelepiped with edges $(1, 0, 2)$, $(2, 1, 0)$ and $(0, 3, 1)$ from one corner, and the volume of the tetrahedron on the same three edges.

11. • For which real $k$ do the vectors $(1, k, 0)$, $(0, 1, k)$ and $(k, 0, 1)$ lie in one plane through the origin?

12. • Find the point where the line $(1, 0, 2) + t(1, -1, 1)$ meets the plane $x + 2y + 3z = 5$.

13. • The planes $x + y + z = 1$ and $x - y + 2z = 0$ meet in a line. Give a vector equation of this line.

14. • Find the distance from the point $(2, 1, 3)$ to the line through the origin with direction $(1, 2, 2)$, in two ways: with the cross product formula, and with the [projection](/0JW13B48YP/) and the Pythagorean theorem.

15. • The lines $t(1, 0, 1)$ and $(1, 1, 0) + s(0, 1, 1)$ are skew. Find the distance between them and the two closest points.

16. • Prove with the dot product that a parallelogram is a rhombus if and only if its diagonals are perpendicular.

17. • Let $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ be four points in space, not necessarily in one plane. Prove that the midpoints of $AB$, $BC$, $CD$ and $DA$ are the vertices of a parallelogram, possibly a flat one.

18. ★ Use the [scalar triple product](/34VJYG6GVD/) and the [vector triple product](/03FTB26QYD/) to prove that $(a \times b) \cdot (c \times d) = (a \cdot c)(b \cdot d) - (a \cdot d)(b \cdot c)$ for all $a, b, c, d \in \R^3$. Deduce that $\norm{a \times b}^2 = \norm{a}^2 \norm{b}^2 - (a \cdot b)^2$.

19. ★ Let $a \neq 0$ in $\R^3$. Prove that the equation $a \times x = b$ has a solution $x$ if and only if $a \cdot b = 0$, and that then the solutions are $x = \dfrac{b \times a}{a \cdot a} + t a$ for $t \in \R$. Solve $(1, 1, 0) \times x = (1, -1, 1)$.

20. ★ Prove with the dot product that the three altitudes of a triangle meet in one point. (Take the meeting point $H$ of two altitudes and show that $H$ lies on the third.)

21. ★ Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be nonzero vectors in $\R^3$ with $a + b + c = 0$, the sides of a triangle taken in order. Prove that $a \times b = b \times c = c \times a$, and deduce the law of sines for the triangle.
