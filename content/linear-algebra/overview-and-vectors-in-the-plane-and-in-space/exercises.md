1. ◦ Which of the following maps $T \colon \R^2 \to \R^2$ satisfy $T(u + v) = T(u) + T(v)$ and $T(cu) = c\,T(u)$ for all $u, v \in \R^2$ and all real $c$? (a) $T(x, y) = (x + y, 2)$. (b) $T(x, y) = (3x - y, x)$. (c) $T(x, y) = (x^2, y)$. For each map that fails, give a pair or a scalar that shows it.

2. ◦ Find the [length](/0XKBKGTCPC/) of $v = (3, -4, 12)$ and the unit vector in the direction of $v$.

3. ◦ Let $u = (1, -2, 2)$ and $v = (3, 0, -4)$. Compute $u \cdot v$ and $\cos\theta$ for the [angle](/0ZEHZY85E6/) $\theta$ between $u$ and $v$. Is $\theta$ acute, right or obtuse?

4. ◦ For which real numbers $t$ are $(t, 2, -1)$ and $(3, t, 4)$ [perpendicular](/03NK6RSRQ1/)?

5. ◦ Compute $(2, 0, 1) \times (1, 3, -1)$, and check with two dot products that the result is perpendicular to both factors.

6. ◦ Find the [projection](/0JW13B48YP/) $p$ of $u = (2, 1, 3)$ along $v = (1, 0, 1)$ and the remainder $u - p$. Check that the remainder is perpendicular to $v$.

7. ◦ The line $L$ is $(2, -1, 0) + t(1, 3, 2)$. Decide whether the points $(4, 5, 4)$ and $(5, 8, 5)$ lie on $L$.

8. ◦ Write the equation of the plane through $(1, 1, 1)$ with normal vector $(2, -1, 3)$ in the form $ax + by + cz = d$, and find the distance from the origin to this plane.

9. ◦ Find the error. A student argues: "$a \times v = a \times w$ with $a \neq 0$, so cancelling $a$ gives $v = w$." Show that the conclusion is false for $a = (1, 0, 0)$, $v = (0, 1, 0)$ and $w = (1, 1, 0)$, and state the correct conclusion that does follow from $a \times v = a \times w$.

10. • Write $u = (4, 1, -2)$ as $p + q$ with $p$ a multiple of $v = (2, 2, 1)$ and $q \perp v$. Check that $\norm{u}^2 = \norm{p}^2 + \norm{q}^2$.

11. • Find the area of the triangle with vertices $(1, 1, 0)$, $(3, 2, 1)$ and $(2, 4, 3)$.

12. • Find the volume of the parallelepiped with edges $(1, 0, 2)$, $(2, 1, 0)$ and $(0, 3, 1)$ from one corner, and the volume of the tetrahedron with the same three edges from one vertex.

13. • For which real $k$ do the vectors $(1, k, 0)$, $(0, 1, k)$ and $(k, 0, 1)$ lie in one plane through the origin?

14. • Find a vector equation of the line in which the planes $x + y + z = 1$ and $x - y + 2z = 0$ meet.

15. • Find the distance from $q = (2, 1, -1)$ to the plane $2x - y + 2z = 3$ and the point of the plane nearest to $q$.

16. • Find the mirror image of the point $(3, 0, 1)$ in the plane $x + y + z = 1$.

17. • Show that the lines $t(1, 0, 1)$ and $(1, 1, 0) + s(0, 1, 1)$ are skew. Find the distance between them and the two points, one on each line, that realize it.

18. • Let $O$ be a corner of the unit cube $[0, 1]^3$. Find the cosine of the angle between the diagonal of the cube from $O$ and an edge from $O$, between that diagonal and a diagonal of a face from $O$, and between two of the four diagonals of the cube. Give each angle to the nearest tenth of a degree.

19. • A parallelogram has sides $a$ and $b$ from one vertex. Prove with the dot product that it is a rhombus, all four sides of equal length, if and only if its diagonals are perpendicular.

20. • Let $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ be four points of the plane. Prove with vectors that the midpoints of $AB$, $BC$, $CD$ and $DA$ are the vertices of a parallelogram, or lie on one line.

21. • Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ and $v = (1, 0)$. Compute $Av$ and $A^2 v$, find numbers $\alpha, \beta$ with $A^2 v = \alpha A v + \beta v$, factor the polynomial $z^2 - \alpha z - \beta$, and use the factors to find an eigenvector of $A$ without computing a determinant.

22. ★ Let $a, b \in \R^3$ with $a \neq 0$. Prove that $a \times x = b$ has a solution $x$ if and only if $a \cdot b = 0$, and that the solutions are then $x = \dfrac{b \times a}{a \cdot a} + t a$ for $t \in \R$. Solve $(1, 1, 0) \times x = (1, -1, 1)$.

23. ★ Prove the Binet-Cauchy identity $(a \times b) \cdot (c \times d) = (a \cdot c)(b \cdot d) - (a \cdot d)(b \cdot c)$ for $a, b, c, d \in \R^3$, using the [vector triple product](/03FTB26QYD/). Deduce $\norm{a \times b}^2 = \norm{a}^2 \norm{b}^2 - (a \cdot b)^2$.

24. ★ Prove that for all $a, b, c \in \R^3$
$$(a \times b) \cdot \bigl((b \times c) \times (c \times a)\bigr) = \bigl(a \cdot (b \times c)\bigr)^2.$$
Deduce that $a \times b$, $b \times c$ and $c \times a$ lie in one plane through $0$ if and only if $a$, $b$ and $c$ do.

25. ★ Prove with the dot product that the three altitudes of a triangle meet in one point.

26. ★ The sides of a triangle, taken as vectors around it, are $a$, $b$, $c$ with $a + b + c = 0$, and the angles opposite them are $A$, $B$, $C$. Show that $a \times b = b \times c = c \times a$, and deduce the law of sines $\dfrac{\sin A}{\norm{a}} = \dfrac{\sin B}{\norm{b}} = \dfrac{\sin C}{\norm{c}}$.
