1. ◦ Which of these subsets are [subspaces](/00YC1D652K/)? (a) $\set{x \in \R^3 : x_1 + 2x_2 - x_3 = 0}$. (b) $\set{x \in \R^3 : x_1 + x_2 = 1}$. (c) $\set{x \in \R^3 : x_1x_2 = 0}$. (d) $\set{x \in \R^3 : x_1 = x_2 = x_3}$. (e) $\set{x \in \R^3 : x_1 \ge 0}$. (f) The $2 \times 2$ real matrices of determinant $0$, in $M_{2 \times 2}(\R)$.

2. ◦ On $\R$, define $x \oplus y = x + y + 1$ and $c \odot x = cx + c - 1$. Assume that these operations make $\R$ a [vector space](/000AYPA9AJ/) over $\R$. Which real number is its zero vector, and what is the negative of $x$? Check that $1 \odot x = x$.

3. ◦ Give the [dimension](/03GSZ8RTZJ/) of each real vector space: (a) $M_{2 \times 3}(\R)$; (b) the polynomials of degree at most $4$; (c) $\C$, viewed as a space over $\R$; (d) the $2 \times 2$ real matrices of trace $0$.

4. ◦ Decide whether $(1, 2, 3)$ and $(1, 2, 4)$ lie in the [span](/0DTY8B9HMS/) of $(1, 0, 1)$ and $(0, 1, 1)$ in $\R^3$.

5. ◦ Use the [column test](/76GG9B25A1/) to decide whether each family is independent, and give a dependence relation when it is not. (a) $(1, 2, 0, 1)$, $(0, 1, 1, 1)$, $(1, 3, 1, 2)$ in $\R^4$. (b) $(1, 0, 2)$, $(2, 1, 0)$, $(0, 1, 1)$ in $\R^3$.

6. ◦ Find the [coordinate vector](/04CYQHN6MC/) of $(3, 1)$ in the ordered basis $B = ((1, 1), (1, -1))$ of $\R^2$, and in the ordered basis $B' = ((1, -1), (1, 1))$.

7. ◦ True or false? (a) Any four vectors in $\R^3$ are linearly dependent. (b) Any three vectors in $\R^3$ span $\R^3$. (c) Five vectors that span a space of dimension $5$ form a basis of it. (d) A subspace of $\R^4$ of dimension $4$ is $\R^4$.

8. ◦ Let $U$ be the $x$-axis and $W$ the $y$-axis in $\R^2$. Show that $U \cup W$ is not a subspace, and give a vector of $U + W$ that is not in $U \cup W$.

9. ◦ In $\mathbb{F}_2^3$, list the vectors of the span of $(1, 1, 0)$ and $(0, 1, 1)$, and decide whether $(1, 1, 1)$ lies in it. How many ordered bases does $\mathbb{F}_3^2$ have?

10. • Let $E$ and $O$ be the even and the odd functions in $\R^{\R}$. Show that both are subspaces, that $E \cap O = \set{0}$, and that $E + O = \R^{\R}$.

11. • Show that no vector space is the union of two proper subspaces. Then show that $\mathbb{F}_2^2$ is the union of three proper subspaces.

12. • Show that $1, x - 1, (x - 1)^2, (x - 1)^3$ is a basis of the polynomials of degree at most $3$, and find the coordinates of $x^3$ in this basis.

13. • Find a basis and the dimension of $W = \set{x \in \R^4 : x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4 = 0, \ x_1 = x_2}$.

14. • For which pairs $\set{e_i, e_j}$ of standard basis vectors of $\R^3$ is $(1, 0, 3), e_i, e_j$ a basis of $\R^3$? State the rule for a general vector $v$ in place of $(1, 0, 3)$.

15. • The vectors $v_1 = (1, 0, 1)$, $v_2 = (2, 0, 2)$, $v_3 = (0, 1, 1)$, $v_4 = (1, 1, 2)$, $v_5 = (1, -1, 0)$ span a subspace $W$ of $\R^3$. Find a basis of $W$ consisting of some of the $v_i$, and give $\dim W$.

16. • In $\R^4$, let $U = \set{x : x_4 = 0}$ and $W = \set{x : x_1 = x_2, \ x_3 = x_4}$. Compute $\dim(U \cap W)$ directly, and use [Grassmann's formula](/06HTJNCMPA/) to show that $U + W = \R^4$.

17. • Show that two subspaces of dimension $3$ in $\R^5$ have a nonzero vector in common. Give two subspaces of dimension $2$ in $\R^4$ that meet only in $0$.

18. • Show that the polynomials $p$ of degree at most $n$ with $p(1) = 0$ form a subspace of dimension $n$, and give a basis.

19. • Find the dimensions of the spaces of symmetric and of skew-symmetric $n \times n$ real matrices, with a basis of each.

20. • Show that $(1, i)$ and $(i, -1)$ are linearly dependent in $\C^2$ over $\C$, and independent when $\C^2$ is viewed as a vector space over $\R$. What is the dimension of $\C^2$ over $\R$?

21. • Let $e_k \in \R^{\N}$ be the sequence with $1$ in place $k$ and $0$ elsewhere. Show that the $e_k$ form a basis of the space of finitely supported sequences, but do not span $\R^{\N}$. Deduce that $\R^{\N}$ is not finite-dimensional.

22. ★ Show that the real numbers $\log p$, for $p$ prime, are linearly independent over $\Q$. Deduce that $\R$ is infinite-dimensional over $\Q$.

23. ★ Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $F$ with at least $n$ elements. Show that $V$ is not the union of $n$ proper subspaces.

24. ★ Show that a vector space of dimension $n$ over a field with $q$ elements has exactly $q^n$ vectors. Deduce that the number of elements of a finite field is a power of a prime.

25. ★ A complex structure on a real vector space $V$ is a linear map $J \colon V \to V$ with $J \circ J = -\mathrm{id}$. Show that if $V$ has finite dimension and a complex structure, then $\dim V$ is even. Conclude that $\R^3$ has no complex structure.

26. ★ Prove directly, without the [Steinitz exchange lemma](/08ZDRN6H41/), that any $n + 1$ vectors in the span of $n$ vectors $w_1, \dots, w_n$ are linearly dependent. Use a homogeneous linear system with more unknowns than equations.
