1. ◦ Write the symmetric matrix of the real [quadratic form](/0RBQS1JJCR/) $Q = 3x^2 - 4xy + 2y^2 + 6yz - z^2$.

2. ◦ Compute the [polar form](/0HYFWKKY3C/) $b_Q(u, v) = Q(u + v) - Q(u) - Q(v)$ of $Q(x, y) = x^2 + 3xy$ on $\R^2$, and the associated symmetric bilinear form $\frac12 b_Q$.

3. ◦ Find the [signature](/07EZA80NEH/) $(p, q, r)$ of (a) $2x^2 - 3y^2 + 5z^2 - w^2$ on $\R^4$; (b) $xy$ on $\R^2$; (c) $(x + y + z)^2$ on $\R^3$.

4. ◦ Give the type (positive or negative definite, positive or negative semidefinite, indefinite) of each form on $\R^2$: $x^2 + 2xy + y^2$, $x^2 + 2xy + 3y^2$, $-x^2 + 4xy - 5y^2$, $x^2 + 6xy + y^2$.

5. ◦ Find the [discriminants](/MZFRXECCMC/) over $\Q$ of $x^2 + 3y^2$, $x^2 + 12y^2$ and $2xy$. Are the first two forms equivalent over $\Q$?

6. ◦ By the [classification over $\C$](/0CEX9BRXRZ/), to which form $x_1^2 + \cdots + x_r^2$ is $x^2 + 2xy + y^2 + z^2$ equivalent over $\C$?

7. ◦ Find the largest and the smallest value of $x^2 + 4xy - 2y^2$ on the unit circle, and the points where they are taken.

8. • Diagonalize $Q = x^2 + 4xy + 4xz + 3y^2 + 2yz + 3z^2$ over $\Q$ by completing squares, and give its signature over $\R$. Check the result with the determinant of the matrix.

9. • Find the signature of the form with matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ in two ways: from the eigenvalues, and by completing squares.

10. • How many equivalence classes of real quadratic forms on $\R^2$ are there, counting degenerate forms? On $\R^n$? Give one representative of each class on $\R^2$.

11. • Is $x^2 + y^2 + z^2$ equivalent to $x^2 + y^2 - z^2$ over $\mathbb F_3$? Over $\mathbb F_5$? Use the theorem on [forms over finite fields](/2HBR7137AD/).

12. • Find a nonzero vector $v \in \mathbb F_7^3$ with $v_1^2 + v_2^2 + v_3^2 = 0$.

13. • Use the [principal axis theorem](/0PR5P9Z4KM/) to find the axes and the lengths of the semi-axes of the ellipse $3x^2 + 2xy + 3y^2 = 8$.

14. • Put the conic $2x^2 + 4xy + 5y^2 - 12x - 24y + 6 = 0$ in normal form: find its centre, its axes and its type.

15. • Find the critical points of $f(x, y) = x^3 - 3xy + y^3$ and classify them by the type of the Hessian form, as in the [second derivative test](/01RBSSGMY5/).

16. • For which real $a$ is $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + 2a(xy + yz + zx)$ positive definite? Positive semidefinite?

17. ★ Let $Q$ be a real quadratic form of signature $(p, q, r)$. Prove that $p$ is the largest dimension of a subspace on which $Q$ is positive definite. Deduce Sylvester's law of inertia.

18. ★ Let $q$ be odd. Show that there are exactly $2n + 1$ equivalence classes of quadratic forms on $\mathbb F_q^n$, counting degenerate forms.

19. ★ Let $A$ be real symmetric with eigenvalues $\lambda_1 \ge \lambda_2 \ge \cdots \ge \lambda_n$ and orthonormal eigenvectors $u_1, \dots, u_n$. Prove that the largest value of $x^T A x$ over unit vectors $x$ orthogonal to $u_1$ is $\lambda_2$. Apply this to $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$.

20. ★ Let $F$ be a field of characteristic not $2$, and let $Q$ be a nondegenerate quadratic form that has an isotropic vector. Prove that $Q$ takes every value of $F$.
