1. ◦ Take the definition "an integer $n$ is a perfect square if $n = k^2$ for some integer $k$". Name the definiendum and the definiens. Is $0$ a perfect square? Is $-4$? Which reading of "if" do your answers use?

2. ◦ With the definition "$a$ divides $b$ if $b = ak$ for some integer $k$", list every integer $a$ that divides $12$, and every integer $b$ that $0$ divides.

3. ◦ Name the [kind](/B60DBNF77S/) of each definition. (a) $\abs{x} = x$ if $x \geq 0$, and $\abs{x} = -x$ otherwise. (b) $F_0 = 0$, $F_1 = 1$ and $F_{n+2} = F_{n+1} + F_n$. (c) The set of balanced strings of brackets is the smallest set that contains the empty string and is closed under $s \mapsto (s)$ and under joining two strings. (d) $e$ is the real number $x$ with $\ln x = 1$. (e) A ring is a set with two operations that satisfy the ring axioms. (f) $\Z/n$ is the set of classes of integers under congruence modulo $n$.

4. ◦ Does the rule $f\left(\frac{a}{b}\right) = a + b$, for integers $a$ and $b \neq 0$, define a function on $\Q$? Give a reason with two fractions.

5. ◦ How many ordered lists of positive primes have product $12$? How many have product $30$? What does the fundamental theorem of arithmetic say about these lists?

6. ◦ Which of these definite descriptions name exactly one object? (a) The real number $x$ with $x^3 = 8$. (b) The real number $x$ with $x^2 = 9$. (c) The integer strictly between $2$ and $5$. (d) The positive real number $x$ with $x^2 = 9$.

7. ◦ The recursion $(n + 1)! = (n + 1) \cdot n!$ holds for all $n \geq 1$ with $1! = 1$. Which value of $0!$ makes it hold at $n = 0$ too? How many ways are there to arrange zero objects in a row?

8. ◦ Find all residues $x$ modulo $6$ with $2x \equiv 4 \pmod 6$. In what sense is the solution unique [up to](/CBC72TX3NQ/) a congruence?

9. • Prove that multiplication of residues, $[a] \cdot [b] := [ab]$, is [well defined](/HNGZEBKVY2/) on $\Z/n$.

10. • For which positive integers $m$ is the rule $[a]_{12} \mapsto [a]_m$ a well-defined map from $\Z/12$ to $\Z/m$? Prove your answer.

11. • Show that the rule $[a]_5 \mapsto [2^a]_5$, for integers $a \geq 0$, is not well defined on $\Z/5$, but that $[a]_4 \mapsto [2^a]_5$ is well defined on $\Z/4$.

12. • Prove that for an integer $n \geq 2$ the following two conditions are [equivalent](/NZ42PS2P1A/): (a) $n$ has no divisor $d$ with $1 < d < n$; (b) $n$ has no divisor $d$ with $1 < d \leq \sqrt n$.

13. • Prove that an integer $n$ is even if and only if $4$ divides $n^2$. Could the second condition serve as a definition of "even"?

14. • Prove that each element $g$ of a [group](/G5S1M9/) has exactly one inverse, so that "the inverse of $g$" is a legal definite description.

15. • For positive integers $a$ and $b$, prove that "the least positive common multiple of $a$ and $b$" names exactly one number.

16. • The Fano plane has seven points $1, \ldots, 7$ and seven lines $\set{1, 2, 3}$, $\set{1, 4, 5}$, $\set{1, 6, 7}$, $\set{2, 4, 6}$, $\set{2, 5, 7}$, $\set{3, 4, 7}$, $\set{3, 5, 6}$. Check that every two points lie on exactly one line and that every two lines meet. What does the model show about the axiom "through a point not on a line there is exactly one line that does not meet it", if "point", "line" and "lies on" are [primitive notions](/QMQEJKFEYZ/) subject only to the incidence axioms?

17. • Suppose $1$ were counted as a prime. Which part of the [fundamental theorem of arithmetic](/084HH1NDCB/) would fail, and why? Then prove that a ring in which $0 = 1$ has exactly one element.

18. • Glue two solid cubes together at a single vertex. Count $V$, $E$ and $F$ for the resulting solid, and compute $V - E + F$. What kind of condition in the definition of a polyhedron would exclude it from [Euler's polyhedron formula](/09HT3P35YP/)?

19. ★ Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$. Prove that the rule $(gH)(kH) := gkH$ is a well-defined operation on the left cosets of $H$ if and only if $H$ is a [normal subgroup](/0CB0DX5BVV/).

20. ★ Let $f \colon A \to B$ be a function with $A$ nonempty. Prove that the following are equivalent with a cycle of three implications: (a) $f$ is injective; (b) there is a function $g \colon B \to A$ with $g(f(a)) = a$ for every $a \in A$; (c) for every set $C$ and all functions $h, k \colon C \to A$, if $f \circ h = f \circ k$, then $h = k$.

21. ★ Let $S$ be the smallest set of integers that contains $0$ and is closed under $n \mapsto n + 3$ and $n \mapsto n - 5$. Prove that $S = \Z$. Then find the smallest set that contains $0$ and is closed under $n \mapsto n + 4$ and $n \mapsto n - 6$, and prove your answer.

22. ★ Prove that every group of order $3$ is isomorphic to $\Z/3$. How many group operations on the set $\set{0, 1, 2}$ have $0$ as their identity?
