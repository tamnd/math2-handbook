1. ◦ Under each of the two [conventions](/CSFEP56A7D/) for "increasing", decide whether $x \mapsto \floor{x}$ is increasing on $\R$, and whether $x \mapsto x^3$ is.

2. ◦ Compute $\sin^2(\pi/6)$. Then give the two readings of $\sin^{-1}(1/2)$, and the value of each to three decimal places.

3. ◦ Evaluate $2 + 3 \cdot 4^2$ and $-3^2 + 6 / 2 \cdot 3$, using the usual precedence rules, with $/$ and $\cdot$ of equal precedence and grouped from the left.

4. ◦ Fill each blank with the right sign among $=$, $:=$, $\equiv$, $\approx$ and $\cong$: (a) $17 \; \_ \; 2 \pmod 5$; (b) $\pi \; \_ \; 3.1416$; (c) $f(x) \; \_ \; x^2 + 1$, as the definition of $f$; (d) $\Z/2 \times \Z/3 \; \_ \; \Z/6$; (e) $(x + 1)^2 \; \_ \; x^2 + 2x + 1$.

5. ◦ Write $x \approx y$ when $\abs{x - y} < 0.1$. Give three numbers $a, b, c$ with $a \approx b$ and $b \approx c$ but not $a \approx c$.

6. ◦ Expand each [abbreviation](/4ZNYMYRRY3/) and rewrite the sentence without it. (a) "If $G$ is abelian (resp. cyclic), then every subgroup of $G$ is abelian (resp. cyclic)." (b) "Wlog $x \leq y$." (c) "NTS $f$ is injective." (d) "TFAE."

7. ◦ At which real numbers does the polynomial $x^3 - x$ vanish?

8. ◦ Write each sum in [sigma notation](/QKEX4PD029/) and evaluate it: (a) $1 + 4 + 9 + \dots + 100$; (b) $1 - 2 + 3 - 4 + \dots + 9 - 10$.

9. ◦ For each statement about $n \in \N$, say whether it holds eventually, frequently but not eventually, or neither: (a) $n^2 > 50n$; (b) $n$ is a multiple of $3$; (c) $n < 1000$; (d) $n! > 3^n$. For each statement that holds eventually, give the least threshold.

10. • For (a), (b) and (d), decide whether the statement about $\N$ is true in the cofinite sense and in the density sense. For (c), decide whether it is true in the countable, the measure and the meagre senses on $\R$. (a) Almost all natural numbers are not perfect squares. (b) Almost all natural numbers are greater than $1000$. (c) Almost all real numbers are not integers. (d) Almost all natural numbers are not multiples of $10$.

11. • Find the least $N$ such that $n! > 10^n$ for every $n \geq N$, and prove that the inequality holds from $N$ on. You may use $24! \approx 6.2 \times 10^{23}$.

12. • Write "the sequence $(a_n)$ is not eventually zero" with quantifiers and without a "not" in front. Decide whether $a_n = \frac{1 + (-1)^n}{2}$ is eventually zero, and whether it is eventually nonzero.

13. • For a function $f \colon \R \to \R$, order these four properties from strongest to weakest, and show that no two of them are equivalent: $f$ is continuous at $0$; $f(x) = x^2$ for all $x$; $f$ is bounded on some open interval around $0$; $f$ is differentiable at $0$.

14. • Find the largest constant $m$ such that $x + \frac1x \geq m$ for all $x \geq 2$. Prove the bound and show that it is [sharp](/KWQV0G0VJ3/).

15. • Find the least constant $C$ such that $\abs{\sin x} \leq C \abs{x}$ for every real $x$. Is the bound attained at some $x \neq 0$?

16. • For a set $X$, write down a bijection from the power set of $X$ to the set of functions $X \to \set{0, 1}$, and explain why it is [canonical](/F8WNADZFMM/). Then count the bijections from $\set{a, b}$ to $\set{0, 1}$, and explain why none of them is canonical.

17. • How many isomorphisms are there from a cyclic group $C_6$ of order $6$ to $\Z/6$? What extra choice singles out one of them?

18. • Following the usual [abuse of notation](/JV68YRB5YZ/), write $3$ for the class $[3]$ in $\Z/11$. Compute $3^{-1}$, $5 \cdot 6$ and $2^{10}$ in $\Z/11$. Which of the statements "$3 = 14$" and "$3 < 5$" makes sense for elements of $\Z/11$?

19. • Decide which statements are true for functions of $n \in \N$, in the sense of [big O](/0TGV80E6A4/): (a) $2^{n+1} = O(2^n)$; (b) $2^{2n} = O(2^n)$; (c) $n = O(n^2)$; (d) $n^2 = O(n)$. Both $n = O(n^2)$ and $n^2 = O(n^2)$ are true. What does this show about the sign $=$ in big O notation?

20. ★ Prove that $n! \geq 2^{n-1}$ for every $n \geq 1$, and that no constant $c > 2$ satisfies $n! \geq c^{n-1}$ for every $n \geq 1$.

21. ★ Prove that the set of numbers in $[0, 1]$ that have a decimal expansion with no digit $7$ has measure zero. In which sense of "almost all" do almost all numbers of $[0, 1]$ contain the digit $7$?

22. ★ Let $f(x) = x$ for rational $x$ and $f(x) = 0$ for irrational $x$. Prove that $f$ is continuous at $0$ and at no other point.

23. ★ Give an [elementary proof](/84SZZBACP3/) that there are infinitely many primes of the form $6k + 5$.

24. ★ Prove that if $a_n \to L$, then for each $\varepsilon > 0$ the set $\set{n : \abs{a_n - L} \geq \varepsilon}$ is finite. Then give a sequence that does not converge, but for which the set $\set{n : \abs{a_n} \geq \varepsilon}$ has density $0$ for every $\varepsilon > 0$.
