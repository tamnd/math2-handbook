18. Remove a vertex $v$ of degree at most $5$ and colour the rest by induction. If the neighbours use all $5$ colours, two non-adjacent neighbours with colours $1$ and $3$ are either not joined by a $1$–$3$ chain, so a swap frees a colour, or they are joined, and the chain separates the neighbours coloured $2$ and $4$. For $4$ colours two swaps are needed at once, and they can interfere, as Heawood showed.
